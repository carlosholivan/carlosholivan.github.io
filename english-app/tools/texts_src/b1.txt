=== 1 | The job interview ¦ La entrevista de trabajo
sum: Una candidata se prepara para una entrevista y repasa las preguntas típicas.
p: Next Monday Laura has an interview for a job at a travel agency. She has never had a real job interview before, so she has asked her friend Mark, who works in human resources, to help her practise.
= El lunes que viene Laura tiene una entrevista para un trabajo en una agencia de viajes. Nunca ha hecho una entrevista de trabajo de verdad, así que le ha pedido a su amigo Mark, que trabaja en recursos humanos, que la ayude a practicar.
p: "Interviewers usually start with easy questions," Mark explains. "They'll ask you what you studied and why you want the job. Then they might ask you to describe yourself. Don't just say you're hard-working and reliable, everybody says that. Give an example of something you have done."
= «Los entrevistadores suelen empezar con preguntas fáciles», explica Mark. «Te preguntarán qué estudiaste y por qué quieres el trabajo. Luego puede que te pidan que te describas. No digas solo que eres trabajadora y fiable; eso lo dice todo el mundo. Pon un ejemplo de algo que hayas hecho».
p: "And at the end they'll say: 'Do you have any questions for us?' Always say yes. You could ask what a typical day is like, or when they will make a decision. It shows that you're interested." Laura writes everything down. "You're very good at this, aren't you?" she says. Mark smiles. "I've interviewed hundreds of people. I know what works."
= «Y al final te dirán: "¿Tiene alguna pregunta para nosotros?". Di siempre que sí. Podrías preguntar cómo es un día normal, o cuándo tomarán una decisión. Demuestra que te interesa». Laura lo apunta todo. «Se te da muy bien esto, ¿verdad?», le dice. Mark sonríe. «He entrevistado a cientos de personas. Sé lo que funciona».
note: what you studied and why you want the job ¦ word-order ¦ Preguntas indirectas: orden de frase afirmativa (what you studied, no what did you study).
note: aren't you ¦ other ¦ Question tag: frase afirmativa con be → tag negativo (You're…, aren't you?).
note: hard-working and reliable ¦ collocation ¦ Adjetivos de personalidad típicos de una entrevista.
note: writes everything down ¦ phrasal ¦ write down = apuntar, anotar.
q: Why has Laura asked Mark for help? ¦ ¿Por qué le ha pedido Laura ayuda a Mark? ¦ He works at the travel agency. / She has never had a job interview. / He is her new boss. ¦ 1 ¦ «She has never had a real job interview before».
q: What advice does Mark give about describing yourself? ¦ ¿Qué consejo da Mark sobre describirse? ¦ Say you're reliable. / Give an example. / Talk about your family. ¦ 1 ¦ «Give an example of something you have done».
q: Why should you ask questions at the end? ¦ ¿Por qué hay que hacer preguntas al final? ¦ It shows you're interested. / It's compulsory. / You can ask about the salary. ¦ 0 ¦ «It shows that you're interested».

=== 2 | The missing painting ¦ El cuadro desaparecido
sum: Una historia de misterio en un museo.
p: It was a quiet Tuesday morning at the City Museum. The guard, Mr Okafor, was walking through the galleries when he noticed something strange. The wall of Room 12 was empty. The museum's most famous painting, a small portrait from the 17th century, had disappeared.
= Era una mañana tranquila de martes en el Museo de la Ciudad. El vigilante, el señor Okafor, iba recorriendo las salas cuando notó algo raro. La pared de la sala 12 estaba vacía. El cuadro más famoso del museo, un pequeño retrato del siglo XVII, había desaparecido.
p: He called the police immediately. When the detectives arrived, they discovered that the alarm had been switched off during the night. Nobody had broken a window or a door. "The thief knew the building very well," said Inspector Hall. "Somebody must have helped him from the inside."
= Llamó a la policía de inmediato. Cuando llegaron los detectives, descubrieron que habían desconectado la alarma durante la noche. Nadie había roto ninguna ventana ni ninguna puerta. «El ladrón conocía muy bien el edificio», dijo la inspectora Hall. «Alguien ha tenido que ayudarle desde dentro».
p: Two days later, the painting was found in the cleaner's car. She admitted that she had taken it, but she said that a man had paid her to do it. The police are still looking for him.
= Dos días después, encontraron el cuadro en el coche de la limpiadora. Admitió que se lo había llevado, pero dijo que un hombre le había pagado para hacerlo. La policía sigue buscándolo.
note: was walking through the galleries when he noticed ¦ tense ¦ Pasado continuo (escena) + pasado simple (acción que la interrumpe).
note: had disappeared ¦ tense ¦ Pasado perfecto: ocurrió antes de que él lo notara.
note: had been switched off ¦ passive ¦ Pasiva en pasado perfecto: had been + participio.
note: She admitted that she had taken it ¦ reported ¦ Estilo indirecto: el pasado simple (took) pasa a pasado perfecto (had taken).
q: What had happened to the painting? ¦ ¿Qué le había pasado al cuadro? ¦ It had been damaged. / It had been stolen. / It had been moved to another room. ¦ 1 ¦ El cuadro había desaparecido: lo robaron.
q: Why did the inspector think someone had helped from the inside? ¦ ¿Por qué pensaba la inspectora que alguien había ayudado desde dentro? ¦ A window was broken. / The alarm was off and nothing was broken. / The guard saw the thief. ¦ 1 ¦ La alarma estaba desconectada y nadie había roto nada.
q: Where was the painting found? ¦ ¿Dónde encontraron el cuadro? ¦ in the museum / in the cleaner's car / in the thief's house ¦ 1 ¦ «the painting was found in the cleaner's car».

=== 3 | Childhood summers ¦ Los veranos de la infancia

note: I wish my children could have ¦ conditional ¦ wish + pasado (could) = deseo sobre el presente.

=== 5 | Learning the hard way ¦ Aprender por las malas

=== 9 | Working from home ¦ Teletrabajar

=== 11 | From bean to cup ¦ Del grano a la taza
sum: Cómo se produce el café que bebemos.
p: Coffee is one of the most popular drinks in the world: about two billion cups are drunk every day. But how is it produced?
= El café es una de las bebidas más populares del mundo: cada día se beben unos dos mil millones de tazas. Pero ¿cómo se produce?
p: Coffee is grown in more than seventy countries, mostly in tropical regions. The beans are actually the seeds of a red fruit called a cherry. The cherries are picked by hand, usually when they are fully ripe. Then the seeds are taken out, dried in the sun and sorted.
= El café se cultiva en más de setenta países, sobre todo en regiones tropicales. Los granos son en realidad las semillas de un fruto rojo llamado cereza. Las cerezas se recogen a mano, normalmente cuando están bien maduras. Luego se sacan las semillas, se secan al sol y se seleccionan.
p: The green beans are exported to countries all over the world, where they are roasted. This is when coffee gets its colour and flavour. Finally, the beans are ground and packaged. In recent years, more attention has been paid to the farmers, who are often badly paid. Fair trade coffee, which guarantees a fair price, can now be found in most supermarkets.
= Los granos verdes se exportan a países de todo el mundo, donde se tuestan. Es entonces cuando el café adquiere su color y su sabor. Por último, los granos se muelen y se envasan. En los últimos años se ha prestado más atención a los agricultores, que a menudo cobran muy poco. El café de comercio justo, que garantiza un precio justo, se puede encontrar hoy en casi todos los supermercados.
note: about two billion cups are drunk every day ¦ passive ¦ Pasiva en presente: lo importante es la acción, no quién la hace.
note: The cherries are picked by hand ¦ passive ¦ Pasiva con complemento de modo (by hand = a mano).
note: more attention has been paid ¦ passive ¦ Pasiva en present perfect: has been + participio.
note: can now be found ¦ passive ¦ Pasiva con modal: can be + participio.
q: What are coffee beans? ¦ ¿Qué son los granos de café? ¦ the seeds of a fruit / small nuts / leaves ¦ 0 ¦ «The beans are actually the seeds of a red fruit».
