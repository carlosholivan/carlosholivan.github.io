=== 1 | A new student ¦ Una alumna nueva ¦ 新しい生徒 ¦ 新同学

=+ Ken: Hola, Lucy. Soy Ken. Encantado de conocerte.

=+ Lucy: No, yo no soy nueva. Este es mi segundo año.

note: the people are ¦ other ¦ people es plural: the people are (✗ the people is). ¦ people は複数扱い：the people are（✗ is）。 ¦ people 是复数：the people are（✗ is）。
note: Marco isn't from Spain ¦ other ¦ Negativa de be: isn't = is not. ¦ be 動詞の否定：isn't＝is not。 ¦ be 动词否定：isn't = is not。

sum: Un abuelo recuerda su primer día de colegio.
p: My grandfather was born in a small village in 1950. His school was very small. There were only twenty children and one teacher. The teacher was very strict, but she was also kind.
= Mi abuelo nació en un pueblo pequeño en 1950. Su colegio era muy pequeño. Solo había veinte niños y una maestra. La maestra era muy estricta, pero también era amable.
p: "On my first day I was very nervous," he says. "I wasn't happy because my mother wasn't there. But the other children were friendly. At the end of the day I wasn't sad. I was tired and hungry!"
= «El primer día estaba muy nervioso», cuenta. «No estaba contento porque mi madre no estaba allí. Pero los otros niños eran simpáticos. Al final del día ya no estaba triste. ¡Estaba cansado y con hambre!».
note: was born ¦ other ¦ Nacer = be born: I was born in 1950 (✗ I born).
note: There were only twenty children ¦ other ¦ Pasado de there are: there were (plural); there was (singular).
note: I wasn't happy ¦ other ¦ Negativa de was: wasn't = was not.
q: When was the grandfather born? ¦ ¿Cuándo nació el abuelo? ¦ in 1915 / in 1950 / in 1960 ¦ 1 ¦ «was born in a small village in 1950».
q: How many teachers were there? ¦ ¿Cuántos maestros había? ¦ one / twenty / two ¦ 0 ¦ «only twenty children and one teacher».
q: How was he at the end of the day? ¦ ¿Cómo estaba al final del día? ¦ sad / tired and hungry / angry ¦ 1 ¦ «I was tired and hungry!».

=== 19 | A weekend in Edinburgh ¦ Un fin de semana en Edimburgo
sum: Laura cuenta su viaje de fin de semana a Edimburgo.
p: Last weekend I went to Edinburgh with my friend Clara. We travelled by train on Friday evening. On Saturday morning we visited the castle and then we walked in the old town. We had lunch in a small café. The soup was delicious!
= El fin de semana pasado fui a Edimburgo con mi amiga Clara. Viajamos en tren el viernes por la tarde. El sábado por la mañana visitamos el castillo y luego paseamos por el casco antiguo. Comimos en una cafetería pequeña. ¡La sopa estaba buenísima!
p: In the afternoon it rained, so we went to a museum. In the evening we saw a play at the theatre. On Sunday we didn't do much. We bought some presents and took the train home. Did I like Edinburgh? Yes, I loved it!
= Por la tarde llovió, así que fuimos a un museo. Por la noche vimos una obra en el teatro. El domingo no hicimos gran cosa. Compramos algunos regalos y cogimos el tren de vuelta a casa. ¿Me gustó Edimburgo? ¡Sí, me encantó!
note: we visited the castle ¦ tense ¦ Pasado simple regular: verbo + -ed (visited, walked, travelled).
note: we saw a play ¦ tense ¦ Pasado irregular: see → saw, go → went, buy → bought, take → took.
note: we didn't do much ¦ tense ¦ Negativa en pasado: didn't + forma base (✗ didn't did).
q: How did they travel to Edinburgh? ¦ ¿Cómo viajaron a Edimburgo? ¦ by plane / by train / by car ¦ 1 ¦ «We travelled by train».
q: Why did they go to a museum? ¦ ¿Por qué fueron a un museo? ¦ because it rained / because it was free / because it was Sunday ¦ 0 ¦ «In the afternoon it rained, so we went to a museum».
q: What did they buy? ¦ ¿Qué compraron? ¦ a book / some presents / tickets for a play ¦ 1 ¦ «We bought some presents».

=== 20 | Big plans ¦ Grandes planes
sum: Tres amigos hablan de sus planes para el verano.
p: Leo: What are you going to do this summer?
+ Nina: I'm going to work in a café in July. I want to save some money. Then in August I'm going to travel to Portugal with my sister.
+ Leo: That sounds great!
+ Nina: And you?
+ Leo: I'm not going to travel. I'm going to stay at home and learn to drive. I'd like to buy a car next year.
+ Sam: I'm going to do nothing! I'd like to sleep all day!
= Leo: ¿Qué vais a hacer este verano?
=+ Nina: Voy a trabajar en una cafetería en julio. Quiero ahorrar algo de dinero. Luego, en agosto, voy a viajar a Portugal con mi hermana.
=+ Leo: ¡Suena genial!
=+ Nina: ¿Y tú?
=+ Leo: Yo no voy a viajar. Me voy a quedar en casa y voy a aprender a conducir. Me gustaría comprarme un coche el año que viene.
=+ Sam: ¡Yo no voy a hacer nada! ¡Me gustaría dormir todo el día!
note: I'm going to work ¦ tense ¦ be going to + verbo: planes ya decididos.
note: I'd like to buy ¦ modal ¦ I'd like to = me gustaría (deseo). I'd = I would.
note: That sounds great ¦ idiom ¦ sound + adjetivo = sonar bien, parecer.
q: Where is Nina going to work? ¦ ¿Dónde va a trabajar Nina? ¦ in a shop / in a café / in Portugal ¦ 1 ¦ «I'm going to work in a café in July».
q: What is Leo going to learn? ¦ ¿Qué va a aprender Leo? ¦ to cook / to drive / Portuguese ¦ 1 ¦ «learn to drive».
q: What would Sam like to do? ¦ ¿Qué le gustaría hacer a Sam? ¦ travel / work / sleep all day ¦ 2 ¦ «I'd like to sleep all day!».
