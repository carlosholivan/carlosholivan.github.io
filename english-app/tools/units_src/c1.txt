=== 1
title: First impressions ¦ Primeras impresiones
desc: Describir personalidad con matices: adjetivos compuestos, intensificadores y lenguaje tentativo
gram: compound adjectives (self-conscious, headstrong) ¦ adverb + adjective collocations (deeply, utterly, mildly) ¦ come across as / strike sb as
ref: c1-personality
words: timid, eccentric, headstrong, tactful, tactless, smug, insecure, withdrawn, candid, conscientious, methodical, ruthless, perceptive, insightful, self-conscious, wary, placid, temperamental, exuberant, cynical, sarcastic, naive, enterprising, dutiful, haughty, quirk, oddity, poise, ego, temperament
intro:
<h3>Hablar de la impresión que da alguien</h3>
<table><tr><td><strong>come across as</strong> + adj./sust.</td><td>He <strong>comes across as</strong> rather smug. (da la impresión de ser)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>strike sb as</strong></td><td>She <strong>struck me as</strong> very perceptive. (me pareció)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>give the impression of</strong> + -ing</td><td>He <strong>gives the impression of being</strong> shy, but he isn't.</td></tr></table>
<h3>Adjetivos compuestos</h3>
<p>Muy productivos en inglés: <b>self-</b> (self-conscious, self-centred), <b>-minded</b> (open-minded, narrow-minded), <b>-tempered</b> (bad-tempered), <b>-going</b> (easy-going), <b>-hearted</b> (kind-hearted).</p>
<h3>Colocaciones adverbio + adjetivo</h3>
<div class="example"><strong>deeply</strong> religious / offended · <strong>utterly</strong> ruthless · <strong>painfully</strong> shy · <strong>mildly</strong> amused · <strong>fiercely</strong> independent · <strong>blissfully</strong> unaware</div>
<div class="tip">En C1 se valora el matiz: en lugar de <i>very shy</i>, usa <i>painfully shy</i>; en lugar de <i>very independent</i>, <i>fiercely independent</i>.</div>
<div class="warning">Falsos amigos: <b>sensible</b> = sensato (sensible en español = <i>sensitive</i>); <b>sympathetic</b> = comprensivo (simpático = <i>nice, friendly</i>); <b>eventual</b> = final.</div>
ex:
fill ¦ He comes ___ as arrogant, but he's actually very shy. ¦ across ¦ over / up / through ¦ Parece arrogante, pero en realidad es muy tímido. ¦ come across as = dar la impresión de.
fill ¦ She struck me ___ someone who doesn't suffer fools gladly. ¦ as ¦ like / for / that ¦ Me pareció alguien que no aguanta a los tontos. ¦ strike sb as.
fill ¦ He's painfully ___ and hardly ever speaks in meetings. ¦ shy ¦ smug / candid / ruthless ¦ Es tremendamente tímido y casi nunca habla en las reuniones. ¦ painfully + shy es una colocación fija.
fill ¦ She's fiercely ___ and hates asking for help. ¦ independent ¦ timid / dutiful / placid ¦ Es ferozmente independiente y odia pedir ayuda. ¦ fiercely independent.
choose ¦ «Es muy sensata» ¦ She's very sensible. ¦ She's very sensitive. / She's very sympathetic. / She's very sensational.
choose ¦ Alguien que dice lo que piensa sin rodeos es… ¦ candid ¦ wary / tactful / withdrawn
order ¦ He gives the impression of being rather headstrong. ¦ Da la impresión de ser bastante testarudo.
order ¦ She was blissfully unaware of the problem. ¦ Era felizmente ajena al problema.
translate ¦ Me pareció muy perspicaz. ¦ She struck me as very perceptive. ¦ He struck me as very perceptive. / She struck me as very insightful. / He struck me as very insightful.
translate ¦ Es demasiado cínico para creer en eso. ¦ He's too cynical to believe in that. ¦ He is too cynical to believe in that. / He's too cynical to believe that.
match ¦ headstrong=testarudo; smug=engreído; wary=receloso; conscientious=concienzudo; placid=plácido

=== 2
title: Little did she know ¦ Poco sabía ella
desc: La inversión tras expresiones negativas y restrictivas para dar énfasis
gram: Never / Rarely / Seldom + auxiliar + sujeto ¦ Not only… but also ¦ No sooner… than / Hardly… when ¦ Only then / Only after / Not until ¦ Little did…
ref: c1-inversion
words: seldom, rarely, scarcely, hardly, barely, little, notwithstanding, beforehand, thereby, startling, staggering, inexplicable, inexplicably, unprecedented, circumstance, profound, profoundly, dismay, astonish, realise, ordinarily, utterly, remotely, outset, under, account, condition, until, only
intro:
<h3>¿Qué es la inversión?</h3>
<p>Cuando una frase empieza por un adverbio <b>negativo o restrictivo</b>, el orden pasa a ser el de una pregunta: <b>auxiliar + sujeto + verbo</b>. Es propia del registro formal y escrito, y da mucho énfasis.</p>
<table><tr><td><strong>Never (before) / Rarely / Seldom</strong></td><td><strong>Never have I seen</strong> such chaos.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Not only … but (also)</strong></td><td><strong>Not only did he</strong> lie, <strong>but</strong> he also blamed me.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>No sooner … than</strong></td><td><strong>No sooner had I</strong> sat down <strong>than</strong> the phone rang.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Hardly / Scarcely / Barely … when</strong></td><td><strong>Hardly had we</strong> arrived <strong>when</strong> it started to rain.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Only then / Only after / Only when</strong></td><td><strong>Only then did I</strong> realise my mistake.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Not until</strong></td><td><strong>Not until</strong> the next day <strong>did she</strong> call.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Little</strong></td><td><strong>Little did she know</strong> what was waiting for her.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Under no circumstances / On no account</strong></td><td><strong>Under no circumstances should you</strong> open this door.</td></tr></table>
<div class="tip">Si no hay auxiliar (presente o pasado simple), se usa <b>do / does / did</b>: <i>Rarely <b>does</b> she complain.</i></div>
<div class="warning">Con <b>Only after / Not until</b> la inversión va en la oración principal, no en la subordinada: ✓ <i>Only after he left <b>did I</b> notice.</i> ✗ <i>Only after did he leave I noticed.</i></div>
ex:
fill ¦ Never ___ I seen such a beautiful sunset. ¦ have ¦ I have / did / was ¦ Nunca había visto una puesta de sol tan bonita. ¦ Never + auxiliar + sujeto.
fill ¦ No sooner had she left ___ it started snowing. ¦ than ¦ when / that / then ¦ Nada más irse ella, empezó a nevar. ¦ No sooner … than.
fill ¦ Little ___ he know that he was being followed. ¦ did ¦ does / was / had ¦ Poco sabía él que lo estaban siguiendo. ¦ Little did + sujeto + know.
fill ¦ Under no circumstances ___ you leave the building. ¦ should ¦ you should / do / must you ¦ Bajo ninguna circunstancia debe salir del edificio. ¦ Under no circumstances + should + sujeto.
choose ¦ Elige la frase correcta. ¦ Not until I got home did I realise I had lost my wallet. ¦ Not until I got home I realised I had lost my wallet. / Not until did I get home I realised I had lost my wallet. / Not until I got home I did realise I had lost my wallet.
choose ¦ «No solo llegó tarde, sino que se olvidó del regalo.» ¦ Not only did he arrive late, but he also forgot the present. ¦ Not only he arrived late, but he also forgot the present. / Not only did he arrived late, but he also forgot the present. / Not only arrived he late, but he also forgot the present.
order ¦ Rarely does she lose her temper. ¦ Rara vez pierde los nervios.
order ¦ Only then did I understand what had happened. ¦ Solo entonces entendí lo que había pasado.
translate ¦ Nunca he visto nada igual. ¦ Never have I seen anything like it. ¦ I have never seen anything like it. / Never have I seen anything like that. / I've never seen anything like it.
translate ¦ Apenas habíamos llegado cuando empezó a llover. ¦ Hardly had we arrived when it started to rain. ¦ Scarcely had we arrived when it started to rain. / Hardly had we arrived when it began to rain. / We had hardly arrived when it started to rain.
match ¦ seldom=rara vez; outset=comienzo; notwithstanding=no obstante; startling=sorprendente; thereby=de ese modo

=== 3
title: What I need is a break ¦ Lo que necesito es un descanso
desc: Oraciones escindidas (cleft sentences) para destacar información
gram: It is/was … that/who ¦ What … is/was ¦ All (that) … is ¦ The thing / reason / place … is ¦ What happened was (that)
ref: c1-cleft
words: emphasis, emphasise, highlight, stress, focus, crucial, vital, essential, mere, merely, solely, precisely, particularly, ultimately, fundamentally, aspect, element, feature, factor, issue, point, matter, concern, irritate, puzzle, frustrate, reason, thing, happen, annoy
intro:
<h3>Partir la frase para dar énfasis</h3>
<p>Una <b>cleft sentence</b> divide una oración en dos partes para poner el foco en un elemento.</p>
<table><tr><td><strong>It is/was X that/who …</strong></td><td>Tom broke the vase → <strong>It was Tom who</strong> broke the vase (no yo).</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>What + sujeto + verbo + is/was …</strong></td><td>I need a holiday → <strong>What I need is</strong> a holiday.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>All (that) … is/was …</strong></td><td><strong>All I want is</strong> some peace and quiet. (lo único que)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>The thing / reason / place / person …</strong></td><td><strong>The reason (why) I left was</strong> the noise.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>What happened was (that) …</strong></td><td><strong>What happened was that</strong> the train broke down.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>What + sujeto + did was …</strong> (acciones)</td><td><strong>What she did was</strong> (to) call the police.</td></tr></table>
<div class="tip">Para destacar una acción usa <b>What … do/did is/was + infinitivo</b>: <i>What you should do <b>is</b> apologise.</i></div>
<div class="warning">«Lo que» al principio de frase es <b>What</b>, no <i>That</i> ni <i>Which</i>: ✗ <i>That I don't understand is…</i> → ✓ <i><b>What</b> I don't understand is…</i></div>
ex:
fill ¦ ___ I don't understand is why she didn't tell us. ¦ What ¦ That / Which / It ¦ Lo que no entiendo es por qué no nos lo dijo. ¦ «Lo que» = What.
fill ¦ It was my sister ___ found the solution, not me. ¦ who ¦ which / what / whom ¦ Fue mi hermana quien encontró la solución, no yo. ¦ It was + persona + who.
fill ¦ ___ I want is a bit of peace and quiet. ¦ All ¦ What all / That / Only ¦ Lo único que quiero es un poco de paz y tranquilidad. ¦ All (that) … is.
fill ¦ What you need to do ___ talk to her directly. ¦ is ¦ it is / does / that ¦ Lo que tienes que hacer es hablar con ella directamente. ¦ What … do is + infinitivo.
choose ¦ Destaca el lugar: «Nos conocimos en París.» ¦ It was in Paris that we met. ¦ It was Paris where we met it. / What we met was in Paris. / Paris was that we met.
choose ¦ «Lo que pasó fue que se fue la luz.» ¦ What happened was that the power went off. ¦ What it happened was the power went off. / That happened was the power went off. / It happened was that the power went off.
order ¦ The reason I left was the noise. ¦ La razón por la que me fui fue el ruido.
order ¦ What really annoys me is his attitude. ¦ Lo que de verdad me molesta es su actitud.
translate ¦ Lo que necesito es dormir. ¦ What I need is sleep. ¦ What I need is some sleep. / What I need is to sleep. / All I need is sleep.
translate ¦ Fue Ana quien llamó. ¦ It was Ana who called. ¦ It was Ana who rang. / It was Ana that called. / It was Ana that rang.
match ¦ merely=simplemente; solely=únicamente; crucial=crucial; puzzle=desconcertar; ultimately=en última instancia

=== 4
title: You needn't have bothered ¦ No hacía falta que te molestaras
desc: Modales avanzados: deducción, obligación en el pasado, crítica y usos especiales
gram: needn't have vs didn't need to ¦ was/were to have done ¦ be bound to / be sure to ¦ might as well ¦ may/might well ¦ should (formal, sorpresa)
ref: c1-modals
words: obligation, necessity, likelihood, probability, bound, plausible, conceivable, inevitable, presumably, supposedly, allegedly, conceivably, doubtless, undoubtedly, deduction, deduce, assume, presume, speculate, speculative, compel, oblige, entitle, permit, exempt, forbid, bother, unnecessary
intro:
<h3>Necesidad en el pasado: dos significados distintos</h3>
<table><tr><td><strong>needn't have</strong> + participio</td><td>You <strong>needn't have bought</strong> wine. (lo compraste, pero no hacía falta)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>didn't need to</strong> + infinitivo</td><td>I <strong>didn't need to</strong> buy wine. (no hacía falta, y normalmente no lo compré)</td></tr></table>
<h3>Probabilidad con matices</h3>
<table><tr><td><strong>be bound to / be sure to</strong></td><td>It's <strong>bound to</strong> rain. (seguro que)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>may/might/could well</strong></td><td>She <strong>may well</strong> be right. (es muy posible)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>might as well / may as well</strong></td><td>The bus has gone; we <strong>might as well</strong> walk. (ya puestos)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>can't have / must have</strong></td><td>He <strong>can't have</strong> forgotten; I reminded him twice.</td></tr></table>
<h3>Planes que no se cumplieron</h3>
<div class="example">We <strong>were to have</strong> married in June, but the wedding was cancelled. (íbamos a…)</div>
<div class="tip"><b>should</b> tras <i>It's strange / odd / surprising that</i> expresa sorpresa: <i>It's odd that he <b>should</b> say that.</i></div>
<div class="warning">«Debe de haber» = <b>must have</b> (deducción), no <i>should have</i> (crítica): <i>She <b>must have</b> left</i> (seguro que se fue) / <i>She <b>should have</b> left</i> (debería haberse ido).</div>
ex:
fill ¦ You ___ have cooked; we'd already eaten. ¦ needn't ¦ didn't need / mustn't / shouldn't to ¦ No hacía falta que cocinaras; ya habíamos comido. ¦ Lo hizo, pero no era necesario → needn't have.
fill ¦ Don't worry, she's ___ to call you back. ¦ bound ¦ sure of / likely of / certain of ¦ No te preocupes, seguro que te devuelve la llamada. ¦ be bound to.
fill ¦ There's nothing on TV. We might as ___ go to bed. ¦ well ¦ good / soon / much ¦ No hay nada en la tele. Ya puestos, nos vamos a la cama. ¦ might as well.
fill ¦ He ___ have taken the keys; they were in my pocket all day. ¦ can't ¦ mustn't / shouldn't / needn't ¦ No puede haberse llevado las llaves; estuvieron en mi bolsillo todo el día. ¦ Deducción negativa → can't have.
choose ¦ «No hacía falta que vinieras (pero viniste).» ¦ You needn't have come. ¦ You didn't need to come. / You mustn't have come. / You couldn't have come.
choose ¦ «Es muy posible que tenga razón.» ¦ She may well be right. ¦ She may as well be right. / She must well be right. / She is bound well to be right.
order ¦ They were to have opened the shop last spring. ¦ Iban a abrir la tienda la primavera pasada.
order ¦ It's strange that he should say such a thing. ¦ Es raro que diga algo así.
translate ¦ Seguro que llega tarde. ¦ He's bound to be late. ¦ He is bound to be late. / She's bound to be late. / She is bound to be late.
translate ¦ No hacía falta que lo compraras. ¦ You needn't have bought it. ¦ You need not have bought it.
match ¦ presumably=presumiblemente; allegedly=supuestamente; inevitable=inevitable; exempt=exento; conceivable=concebible

=== 5
title: Getting things done ¦ Encargar que te hagan las cosas
desc: Pasiva avanzada y estructuras causativas
gram: have/get sth done ¦ have sb do / get sb to do ¦ pasiva con verbos de opinión (is said to have) ¦ pasiva de infinitivo y gerundio (to be done, being done) ¦ need + -ing
ref: c1-passive-causative
words: refurbish, refurbishment, renovate, repair, install, plumbing, plumber, carpenter, contractor, invoice, estimate, quote, deadline, specification, outsource, delegate, appliance, malfunction, maintenance, overhaul, inspect, inspection, decorate, plaster, premises, alleged, reportedly, rumour, persuade, convince
intro:
<h3>Estructuras causativas</h3>
<table><tr><td><strong>have / get</strong> + objeto + participio</td><td>I <strong>had my car repaired</strong>. (me lo arreglaron)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>have</strong> + persona + infinitivo sin to</td><td>I'll <strong>have the plumber look</strong> at it.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>get</strong> + persona + <strong>to</strong> + infinitivo</td><td>I <strong>got my brother to help</strong> me. (conseguí que)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>have sth done</strong> (algo malo que te pasa)</td><td>She <strong>had her bag stolen</strong>. (le robaron el bolso)</td></tr></table>
<h3>Pasiva con verbos de opinión</h3>
<p>Muy usada en noticias: <b>It is said / believed / thought / reported that…</b> o <b>Sujeto + is said to + infinitivo</b>.</p>
<div class="example">The minister <strong>is believed to be</strong> abroad. (presente)<br>The thieves <strong>are thought to have escaped</strong> by car. (pasado → to have + participio)</div>
<h3>Infinitivo y gerundio pasivos</h3>
<div class="example">I hate <strong>being interrupted</strong>. · She expects <strong>to be paid</strong> on time. · The house <strong>needs painting</strong> (= needs to be painted).</div>
<div class="warning">«Me corté el pelo» en la peluquería es <i>I <b>had</b> my hair cut</i>; <i>I cut my hair</i> significa que lo hiciste tú.</div>
ex:
fill ¦ We're having the kitchen ___ next month. ¦ refurbished ¦ refurbish / refurbishing / to refurbish ¦ Nos van a reformar la cocina el mes que viene. ¦ have + objeto + participio.
fill ¦ I finally got the landlord ___ the boiler. ¦ to fix ¦ fix / fixed / fixing ¦ Por fin conseguí que el casero arreglara la caldera. ¦ get + persona + to + infinitivo.
fill ¦ The painting is said ___ been stolen in 1990. ¦ to have ¦ to / having / that it has ¦ Se dice que el cuadro fue robado en 1990. ¦ Pasado → to have + participio.
fill ¦ This shirt needs ___ . ¦ ironing ¦ iron / to iron / ironed ¦ Esta camisa necesita plancharse. ¦ need + -ing = sentido pasivo.
choose ¦ «Le robaron la cartera en el metro.» ¦ He had his wallet stolen on the underground. ¦ He stole his wallet on the underground. / He got stolen his wallet on the underground. / His wallet had him stolen on the underground.
choose ¦ «Se cree que el presidente está enfermo.» ¦ The president is believed to be ill. ¦ The president is believed being ill. / It believes that the president is ill. / The president believes to be ill.
order ¦ I hate being told what to do. ¦ Odio que me digan lo que tengo que hacer.
order ¦ We had the roof inspected before buying the house. ¦ Hicimos que inspeccionaran el tejado antes de comprar la casa.
translate ¦ Me corté el pelo ayer (en la peluquería). ¦ I had my hair cut yesterday. ¦ I got my hair cut yesterday.
translate ¦ Se dice que es el mejor restaurante de la ciudad. ¦ It is said to be the best restaurant in the city. ¦ It's said to be the best restaurant in the city. / It is said to be the best restaurant in town. / It's said to be the best restaurant in town.
match ¦ invoice=factura; contractor=contratista; overhaul=revisión a fondo; premises=local; plumbing=fontanería

=== 6
title: Had I known ¦ De haberlo sabido
desc: Condicionales avanzadas: inversión, alternativas a if y condicionales implícitas
gram: Had / Should / Were + sujeto (inversión) ¦ provided / providing / as long as / on condition that ¦ but for / otherwise / supposing ¦ if it weren't for / if it hadn't been for
ref: c1-conditionals
words: condition, provided, providing, supposing, otherwise, unless, assuming, regardless, hypothetical, scenario, consequence, outcome, alternatively, conditionally, eventuality, contingency, regret, hindsight, foresee, foresight, prevent, avert, fortunate, fortunately, luckily, disaster, crisis, risk, chance
intro:
<h3>Condicionales con inversión (formal)</h3>
<p>Se elimina <i>if</i> y se invierte el auxiliar:</p>
<table><tr><td><strong>Had I known</strong>, I would have come.</td><td>= If I had known…</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Should you need</strong> help, call me.</td><td>= If you (should) need… (formal, por si acaso)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Were I</strong> in your position, I'd resign.</td><td>= If I were…</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Were he to</strong> find out, he'd be furious.</td><td>= If he found out…</td></tr></table>
<h3>Alternativas a if</h3>
<div class="example"><strong>Provided / Providing (that)</strong> you pay on time, there's no problem.<br><strong>As long as / So long as</strong> you're happy, I'm happy.<br><strong>On condition that</strong> he apologises.<br><strong>Supposing</strong> you lost your job, what would you do?<br><strong>But for</strong> your help, we'd have failed. (de no ser por)<br>Hurry up, <strong>otherwise</strong> we'll miss the train.</div>
<div class="tip"><b>If it weren't for</b> (presente) / <b>If it hadn't been for</b> (pasado) = de no ser por. Con inversión: <i><b>Had it not been for</b> the rain…</i></div>
<div class="warning">En la inversión negativa <b>not</b> va detrás del sujeto y sin contracción: ✓ <i>Had I <b>not</b> seen it…</i> ✗ <i>Hadn't I seen it…</i></div>
ex:
fill ¦ ___ I known about the strike, I would have taken the car. ¦ Had ¦ If / Have / Should ¦ De haber sabido lo de la huelga, habría cogido el coche. ¦ Inversión: Had + sujeto + participio.
fill ¦ ___ you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us. ¦ Should ¦ Would / Had / Were ¦ Si tiene alguna pregunta, no dude en ponerse en contacto con nosotros. ¦ Should + sujeto = por si (formal).
fill ¦ You can borrow the car ___ you bring it back by six. ¦ provided ¦ unless / otherwise / supposing ¦ Puedes llevarte el coche siempre que lo devuelvas antes de las seis. ¦ provided (that) = siempre que.
fill ¦ ___ for your advice, I'd have made a terrible mistake. ¦ But ¦ If / Without / Unless ¦ De no ser por tu consejo, habría cometido un error terrible. ¦ But for + sustantivo.
choose ¦ «De no haber sido por la lluvia, habríamos ganado.» ¦ Had it not been for the rain, we would have won. ¦ Hadn't it been for the rain, we would have won. / If it wasn't for the rain, we would win. / Had not it been for the rain, we would have won.
choose ¦ «Si yo estuviera en tu lugar, aceptaría.» (formal) ¦ Were I in your place, I would accept. ¦ Was I in your place, I would accept. / Had I in your place, I would accept. / Would I be in your place, I would accept.
order ¦ Supposing you won the lottery, what would you do? ¦ Suponiendo que ganaras la lotería, ¿qué harías?
order ¦ Leave now, otherwise you'll miss the last bus. ¦ Vete ya; si no, perderás el último autobús.
translate ¦ De haberlo sabido, te habría llamado. ¦ Had I known, I would have called you. ¦ If I had known, I would have called you. / Had I known, I'd have called you. / If I'd known, I'd have called you.
translate ¦ Siempre que me avises, no hay problema. ¦ As long as you let me know, there's no problem. ¦ Provided you let me know, there's no problem. / As long as you tell me, there's no problem. / Providing you let me know, there's no problem.
match ¦ hindsight=retrospectiva; avert=evitar; contingency=imprevisto; foresee=prever; scenario=escenario

=== 7
title: Bringing it up ¦ Sacar el tema
desc: Verbos frasales de nivel avanzado en contextos de trabajo y relaciones
gram: phrasal verbs separables e inseparables ¦ phrasal verbs de tres partes (put up with, come up with) ¦ sustantivos derivados (breakdown, outcome, setback)
ref: c1-phrasal-verbs
words: bring up, come up with, put up with, look into, figure out, turn down, carry out, set up, break down, fall out, go through, get across, live up to, run into, back up, rule out, phase out, call off, put off, catch on, brush up on, iron out, play down, step down, sort out, take over, wind up, drop out, setback, outcome
intro:
<h3>Separables e inseparables</h3>
<p>Con los <b>separables</b>, si el objeto es un pronombre va en medio: <i>turn it down</i> (✗ <i>turn down it</i>). Los <b>inseparables</b> nunca se parten: <i>look into it</i>, <i>run into her</i>.</p>
<table><tr><td><strong>bring up</strong></td><td>sacar (un tema); criar</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>rule out</strong></td><td>descartar</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>iron out</strong></td><td>limar (problemas)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>play down</strong></td><td>quitar importancia</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>phase out</strong></td><td>eliminar gradualmente</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>step down</strong></td><td>dimitir, apartarse</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>live up to</strong></td><td>estar a la altura de</td></tr>

title: Not that I mind ¦ No es que me importe
desc: Recursos de énfasis y actitud: do enfático, adverbios de comentario, so/such, whatever/however
gram: do / does / did enfático ¦ adverbios de comentario (frankly, admittedly, understandably) ¦ so + adj. / such (a) + adj. + sust. ¦ whatever / whoever / however ¦ not that…
ref: c1-attitude
words: frankly, honestly, admittedly, understandably, predictably, regrettably, surprisingly, interestingly, curiously, ironically, thankfully, hopefully, undoubtedly, evidently, obviously, clearly, naturally, whatever, whoever, however, wherever, whenever, attitude, stance, viewpoint, emphatic, insist, mind, matter
intro:
<h3>Do enfático</h3>
<p>En afirmativa, <b>do / does / did</b> + infinitivo refuerza o contradice: <i>I <b>do</b> like your dress!</i> (¡me encanta de verdad!) · <i>She <b>did</b> call; you just didn't hear it.</i></p>
<h3>Adverbios de comentario</h3>
<p>Muestran tu actitud ante toda la frase y suelen ir al principio con coma:</p>
<div class="example"><strong>Frankly</strong>, I don't care. · <strong>Understandably</strong>, she was upset. · <strong>Ironically</strong>, the fire station burnt down. · <strong>Regrettably</strong>, we cannot accept your offer.</div>
<h3>so / such</h3>
<div class="example">It was <strong>so</strong> cold (that) we stayed in. · It was <strong>such a</strong> cold day (that)… · <strong>such</strong> beautiful weather (incontable, sin <i>a</i>)</div>
<h3>whatever / however / whoever</h3>
<div class="example"><strong>Whatever</strong> you do, don't tell her. (hagas lo que hagas)<br><strong>However</strong> hard I try, I can't remember. (por mucho que)<br><strong>Whoever</strong> wins, it'll be a great match.</div>
<div class="tip"><b>Not that…</b> introduce una aclaración: <i>He didn't invite me — <b>not that</b> I wanted to go.</i> (no es que quisiera ir)</div>
<div class="warning">✗ <i>such cold weather day</i>; ✓ <i>such a cold day</i> / <i>such cold weather</i>. <i>Such</i> necesita sustantivo; <i>so</i>, adjetivo o adverbio solos.</div>

title: I'd rather you didn't ¦ Preferiría que no
desc: Pasado irreal y deseos: would rather, it's time, as if, if only, wish
gram: would rather + sujeto + pasado ¦ it's (high/about) time + pasado ¦ as if / as though ¦ wish / if only + pasado / pasado perfecto / would ¦ suppose / imagine + pasado

intro:
<h3>Pasado con valor de presente</h3>
<table><tr><td><strong>would rather</strong> + sujeto + pasado</td><td>I'<strong>d rather you didn't</strong> smoke here. (preferiría que no)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>it's (high / about) time</strong> + sujeto + pasado</td><td><strong>It's high time</strong> you <strong>got</strong> a job. (ya va siendo hora)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>as if / as though</strong> + pasado</td><td>He talks <strong>as if</strong> he <strong>knew</strong> everything.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>suppose / imagine</strong> + pasado</td><td><strong>Suppose</strong> you <strong>lost</strong> your job…</td></tr></table>
<h3>wish / if only</h3>
<table><tr><td>+ pasado (presente irreal)</td><td>I <strong>wish</strong> I <strong>had</strong> more time.</td></tr>
<tr><td>+ pasado perfecto (lamento pasado)</td><td><strong>If only</strong> I <strong>had listened</strong> to you!</td></tr>
<tr><td>+ would (queja sobre otros)</td><td>I <strong>wish</strong> you <strong>would</strong> stop interrupting.</td></tr></table>
<div class="tip">Con el mismo sujeto, <b>would rather</b> va con infinitivo sin to: <i>I'd rather <b>stay</b> at home.</i> Con sujeto distinto, pasado: <i>I'd rather <b>you stayed</b>.</i></div>
<div class="warning">No se dice <i>I wish I would…</i> para uno mismo: ✗ <i>I wish I would be taller</i> → ✓ <i>I wish I <b>were</b> taller</i>.</div>
