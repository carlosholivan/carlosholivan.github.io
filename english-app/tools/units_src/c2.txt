# C2 lessons (EOI Nivel Avanzado C2). Spanish only.

=== 1
title: I was wondering if… ¦ Me preguntaba si…
desc: Tiempos verbales para distanciarse y suavizar: cortesía, tacto y peticiones delicadas
gram: distancing past (I was wondering, I wanted to ask) ¦ polite continuous (I'm hoping, I'd been hoping) ¦ would / might / could to soften ¦ I would have thought…
ref: c2-distancing
words: tact, tactful, tactfully, diplomacy, diplomatic, courteous, courtesy, tentative, tentatively, deferential, imposition, impose, presume, presumptuous, intrusive, request, favour, inconvenience, wonder, hope, think, mind, ask, indirect, indirectly, politeness, gauche, ingratiating, haughty
intro:
<h3>El pasado que no es pasado</h3>
<p>En inglés, alejar el verbo en el tiempo (presente → pasado, simple → continuo) lo aleja también del interlocutor: la petición suena menos directa y más cortés.</p>
<table><tr><td>I wonder if you could help me.</td><td>cortés</td></tr>
<tr><td>I <strong>was wondering</strong> if you could help me.</td><td>más cortés</td></tr>
<tr><td>I <strong>was wondering</strong> if you <strong>might</strong> be able to help me.</td><td>muy cortés, casi tímido</td></tr>
<tr><td>We <strong>were hoping</strong> you'd join us.</td><td>invitación suave</td></tr>
<tr><td>I'<strong>d been hoping</strong> to talk to you about…</td><td>introducción de un tema delicado</td></tr></table>
<h3>Suavizar desacuerdos y críticas</h3>
<div class="example"><strong>I would have thought</strong> that was obvious. (pues yo diría que…)<br><strong>I'd have to say</strong> I don't entirely agree.<br><strong>It might be worth</strong> reconsidering the budget.<br><strong>Wouldn't it be better</strong> to wait?</div>
<div class="tip">Combina mecanismos para graduar la cortesía: pasado + continuo + modal + <i>just / possibly</i>: <i>I <b>was just wondering</b> whether you <b>could possibly</b>…</i></div>
<div class="warning">El exceso de rodeos también comunica algo: en un contexto de confianza puede sonar frío o irónico. A nivel C2 se espera que elijas el grado adecuado a cada situación.</div>
ex:
fill ¦ I was ___ whether you might be free on Thursday. ¦ wondering ¦ wonder / wondered / to wonder ¦ Me preguntaba si estarías libre el jueves. ¦ Pasado continuo de cortesía.
fill ¦ We ___ hoping you'd say a few words at the dinner. ¦ were ¦ are being / had / would ¦ Esperábamos que dijeras unas palabras en la cena. ¦ were hoping = petición suave.
fill ¦ I would have ___ that was the manager's responsibility. ¦ thought ¦ think / thinking / to think ¦ Yo diría que eso era responsabilidad del gerente. ¦ I would have thought = desacuerdo suavizado.
fill ¦ It might be ___ checking the figures again. ¦ worth ¦ worthy / value / good ¦ Quizá valdría la pena revisar las cifras otra vez. ¦ It might be worth + -ing.
choose ¦ La petición más cortés: ¦ I was just wondering if you could possibly lend me your notes. ¦ Lend me your notes. / Can you lend me your notes? / I want you to lend me your notes.
choose ¦ Un comentario «gauche» es… ¦ torpe y falto de tacto ¦ muy cortés / muy irónico / excesivamente formal
order ¦ I'd been hoping to have a word with you about the contract. ¦ Quería hablar contigo sobre el contrato.
order ¦ Wouldn't it be better to wait until Monday? ¦ ¿No sería mejor esperar al lunes?
translate ¦ Me preguntaba si podría ayudarme. ¦ I was wondering if you could help me. ¦ I was wondering whether you could help me. / I was wondering if you might help me. / I was wondering if you could possibly help me.
translate ¦ No quisiera ser indiscreto, pero… ¦ I don't mean to intrude, but… ¦ I don't want to intrude, but… / I don't wish to intrude, but… / I don't mean to pry, but…
match ¦ presumptuous=atrevido, presuntuoso; deferential=respetuoso, deferente; imposition=abuso, molestia; courteous=cortés; intrusive=entrometido

=== 2
title: Be that as it may ¦ Sea como fuere
desc: El subjuntivo y las fórmulas fijas del registro formal
gram: subjunctive after verbs and adjectives of demand (insist that he be) ¦ fixed phrases: be that as it may, come what may, so be it, suffice it to say ¦ lest + subjunctive or should
ref: c2-subjunctive
words: insist, demand, require, stipulate, stipulation, recommend, propose, essential, imperative, vital, mandatory, obligatory, crucial, lest, suffice, heaven, forbid, behest, decree, ordain, adhere, compliance, binding, clause, contractual, provision, unequivocal, unequivocally, paramount, stringent
intro:
<h3>El subjuntivo en inglés</h3>
<p>Es la forma base del verbo, igual para todas las personas (sin -s, sin pasado): <b>be</b>, <b>have</b>, <b>go</b>. Aparece tras verbos y adjetivos de exigencia, sobre todo en registro formal y en inglés americano.</p>
<table><tr><td>insist / demand / recommend / propose / require <strong>that</strong></td><td>They insisted that he <strong>be</strong> present.</td></tr>
<tr><td>It is essential / vital / imperative / crucial <strong>that</strong></td><td>It is vital that she <strong>have</strong> access to a lawyer.</td></tr>
<tr><td>negativa: <strong>not</strong> + base</td><td>We recommend that the item <strong>not be</strong> used.</td></tr></table>
<div class="tip">En inglés británico es frecuente la alternativa con <b>should</b>: <i>It is essential that he <b>should be</b> informed.</i> Las dos son correctas.</div>
<h3>Fórmulas fijas con subjuntivo</h3>
<div class="example"><strong>Be that as it may</strong>, we must decide today. (sea como fuere)<br>I'll be there, <strong>come what may</strong>. (pase lo que pase)<br>If that's your decision, <strong>so be it</strong>. (que así sea)<br><strong>Suffice it to say</strong> (that) we won't be going back. (baste decir que)<br><strong>Heaven forbid</strong> that anything should go wrong. (Dios no lo quiera)<br>She spoke quietly <strong>lest</strong> she <strong>wake</strong> the baby. (para no, por miedo a)</div>
<div class="warning">✗ <i>It's essential that he is there</i> no es incorrecto en el habla británica, pero en un texto formal de C2 se espera <i>that he <b>be</b> there</i> o <i>that he <b>should be</b> there</i>.</div>
ex:
fill ¦ The committee insisted that the report ___ published immediately. ¦ be ¦ is / was / would be ¦ El comité insistió en que el informe se publicara de inmediato. ¦ Subjuntivo: be para todas las personas.
fill ¦ It is imperative that every passenger ___ a valid ticket. ¦ have ¦ has / had / having ¦ Es imprescindible que todos los pasajeros tengan un billete válido. ¦ Subjuntivo: have (sin -s).
fill ¦ Be that as it ___ , the decision has been made. ¦ may ¦ might / can / will ¦ Sea como fuere, la decisión ya está tomada. ¦ Fórmula fija.
fill ¦ ___ it to say, I won't be eating there again. ¦ Suffice ¦ Enough / Sufficient / Suffices ¦ Baste decir que no volveré a comer allí. ¦ Suffice it to say.
choose ¦ «Recomendamos que no se utilice el producto.» ¦ We recommend that the product not be used. ¦ We recommend that the product isn't used to. / We recommend that the product doesn't be used. / We recommend the product not to be using.
choose ¦ «Pase lo que pase» ¦ come what may ¦ what may come / come it may / be what come
order ¦ She whispered lest anyone hear her. ¦ Susurró para que nadie la oyera.
order ¦ It is essential that he be informed at once. ¦ Es esencial que se le informe de inmediato.
translate ¦ Si esa es tu decisión, que así sea. ¦ If that is your decision, so be it. ¦ If that's your decision, so be it.
translate ¦ Exigieron que dimitiera. ¦ They demanded that he resign. ¦ They demanded that he should resign. / They demanded that she resign. / They demanded his resignation. / They demanded that she should resign.
match ¦ stipulate=estipular; paramount=primordial; stringent=riguroso; lest=para que no; behest=petición, orden

=== 3
title: Such was the noise ¦ Tal era el ruido
desc: Anteposición (fronting) y otras inversiones de estilo
gram: Such was / were… that ¦ So + adj. + was… that ¦ inversion after expressions of place (Into the room walked…) ¦ fronting (This I cannot accept) ¦ inversion in comparisons (as did his father)
ref: c2-fronting
words: such, so, inversion, stylistic, stylistically, rhetoric, rhetorical, emphatic, dramatic, effect, prominence, consternation, uproar, tumult, din, deafening, astonishment, incredulity, aghast, elated, jubilant, crowd, roar, hush, gasp, ensue, bewilder, bewildered
intro:
<h3>Such / So al principio</h3>
<table><tr><td><strong>Such was</strong> + sustantivo + <strong>that</strong></td><td><strong>Such was the noise that</strong> nobody heard the alarm.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>So</strong> + adj. + <strong>was/were</strong> + sujeto + <strong>that</strong></td><td><strong>So loud was the noise that</strong> nobody heard the alarm.</td></tr></table>
<h3>Inversión tras expresiones de lugar</h3>
<p>Típica de la narración: el verbo (de movimiento o posición) va delante del sujeto.</p>
<div class="example"><strong>Into the room walked</strong> a tall woman in black.<br><strong>At the top of the hill stands</strong> an old church.<br><strong>Here comes</strong> the bus. · <strong>There goes</strong> my weekend!</div>
<div class="tip">Si el sujeto es un pronombre no hay inversión: <i>Here <b>it comes</b></i>, no <i>Here comes it</i>.</div>
<h3>Anteponer el objeto o un complemento</h3>
<div class="example"><strong>This</strong> I cannot accept. · <strong>What he did next</strong> nobody could have predicted. · <strong>Try as I might</strong>, I couldn't open it. (por más que lo intentara)</div>
<h3>Inversión tras as / than en comparaciones (formal)</h3>
<div class="example">She studied law, <strong>as did</strong> her father. · Young people spend more online <strong>than do</strong> their parents.</div>
<div class="warning">Es un recurso de estilo: úsalo en textos escritos o narrativos, no en una conversación informal, donde sonaría afectado.</div>
ex:
fill ¦ ___ was her anger that she couldn't speak. ¦ Such ¦ So / Too / Very ¦ Tal era su enfado que no podía hablar. ¦ Such was + sustantivo + that.
fill ¦ So ___ was the film that half the audience left. ¦ boring ¦ bored / boredom / bore ¦ Tan aburrida era la película que la mitad del público se marchó. ¦ So + adjetivo + was.
fill ¦ Into the hall ___ the bride and groom. ¦ came ¦ come they / did come / were coming they ¦ Al salón entraron los novios. ¦ Inversión tras expresión de lugar.
fill ¦ Try as I ___ , I couldn't remember his name. ¦ might ¦ may to / could have / did ¦ Por más que lo intentaba, no podía recordar su nombre. ¦ Try as I might.
choose ¦ Elige la frase correcta. ¦ Here it comes. ¦ Here comes it. / Here does it come. / Here it does come.
choose ¦ «Estudió medicina, igual que su madre.» (formal) ¦ He studied medicine, as did his mother. ¦ He studied medicine, as his mother did it. / He studied medicine, like did his mother. / He studied medicine, as had his mother done.
order ¦ At the end of the garden stood an old oak tree. ¦ Al fondo del jardín había un viejo roble.
order ¦ Such was the din that we had to shout. ¦ Tal era el estruendo que teníamos que gritar.
translate ¦ Tan grande era la multitud que cerraron las puertas. ¦ So large was the crowd that they closed the doors. ¦ So big was the crowd that they closed the doors. / Such was the crowd that they closed the doors. / So large was the crowd that the doors were closed.
translate ¦ Esto no lo puedo aceptar. ¦ This I cannot accept. ¦ This I can't accept. / I cannot accept this. / I can't accept this.
match ¦ din=estruendo; hush=silencio; aghast=horrorizado; elated=eufórico; ensue=seguir, resultar

=== 4
title: All things considered ¦ Bien mirado
desc: Cláusulas absolutas y de participio en el registro culto
gram: absolute clauses (Weather permitting, The meeting over, …) ¦ perfect and passive participles (Having been warned…) ¦ fixed phrases: all things considered, that said, generally speaking, given the circumstances
ref: c2-absolute
words: absolute, clause, participle, permit, weather, generally, broadly, strictly, roughly, frankly, notwithstanding, hitherto, thereafter, whereupon, forthwith, henceforth, aforementioned, subsequent, adjourn, recess, deliberation, verdict, testimony
intro:
<h3>Cláusulas absolutas</h3>
<p>Tienen su <b>propio sujeto</b> y un participio (o un adjetivo/preposición), sin verbo conjugado. Son muy concisas y formales.</p>
<div class="example"><strong>Weather permitting</strong>, the ceremony will be held outdoors. (si el tiempo lo permite)<br><strong>The meeting over</strong>, we went for a drink. (terminada la reunión)<br><strong>Her voice trembling</strong>, she read the letter aloud.<br><strong>All things considered</strong>, it wasn't a bad year. (bien mirado)</div>
<h3>Participios perfectos y pasivos</h3>
<table><tr><td><strong>Having + participio</strong></td><td><strong>Having finished</strong> the report, she went home.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Having been + participio</strong></td><td><strong>Having been warned</strong> twice, he had no excuse.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Not having + participio</strong></td><td><strong>Not having read</strong> the book, I kept quiet.</td></tr></table>
<h3>Fórmulas de comentario</h3>
<div class="example"><strong>That said</strong>, … (dicho esto) · <strong>Generally / Broadly / Strictly speaking</strong> · <strong>Judging by</strong> his face… · <strong>Given</strong> the circumstances… · <strong>Considering</strong> his age…</div>
<div class="warning">Cuidado con el participio «colgante»: el sujeto implícito debe ser el de la oración principal. ✗ <i>Walking down the street, the trees looked beautiful</i> (¡los árboles no caminan!). ✓ <i>Walking down the street, <b>I</b> noticed how beautiful the trees looked.</i></div>
ex:
fill ¦ Weather ___ , we'll have lunch in the garden. ¦ permitting ¦ permitted / permits / to permit ¦ Si el tiempo lo permite, comeremos en el jardín. ¦ Cláusula absoluta fija.
fill ¦ ___ been told the truth, she was furious. ¦ Having ¦ Being / Had / Have ¦ Al haberle contado la verdad, se puso furiosa. ¦ Having been + participio.
fill ¦ All things ___ , the trip was a success. ¦ considered ¦ considering / consider / to consider ¦ Bien mirado, el viaje fue un éxito. ¦ All things considered.
fill ¦ The work ___ , they sat down to rest. ¦ done ¦ doing / did / does ¦ Terminado el trabajo, se sentaron a descansar. ¦ Cláusula absoluta: sujeto + participio.
choose ¦ Elige la frase correcta. ¦ Arriving late, I found the door locked. ¦ Arriving late, the door was locked. / Arriving late, the door locked me. / Having arriving late, I found the door locked.
choose ¦ «Dicho esto, …» ¦ That said, … ¦ This saying, … / Having said it this, … / Said that, …
order ¦ Strictly speaking, this is not a legal requirement. ¦ En sentido estricto, esto no es un requisito legal.
order ¦ Not having been invited, we stayed at home. ¦ Como no nos habían invitado, nos quedamos en casa.
translate ¦ Si el tiempo lo permite, saldremos temprano. ¦ Weather permitting, we'll leave early. ¦ Weather permitting, we will leave early. / Weather permitting, we'll set off early. / Weather permitting, we will set off early.
translate ¦ Terminada la cena, salimos a pasear. ¦ Dinner over, we went for a walk. ¦ Dinner finished, we went for a walk. / Having finished dinner, we went for a walk. / Having had dinner, we went for a walk.
match ¦ hitherto=hasta ahora; henceforth=de ahora en adelante; whereupon=tras lo cual; forthwith=de inmediato; adjourn=aplazar, levantar (sesión)

=== 5
title: A long-overdue, carefully worded apology ¦ Una disculpa muy esperada y cuidadosamente redactada
desc: Sintagmas nominales complejos: premodificación, posmodificación y densidad informativa
gram: compound adjectives before the noun (a five-year-old boy) ¦ noun + noun (a government health campaign) ¦ postmodification with a preposition, participle or infinitive (the man to ask) ¦ apposition
ref: c2-noun-phrases
words: compound, density, concise, overdue, long-standing, far-reaching, deep-rooted, wide-ranging, ill-advised, well-founded, hard-won, short-lived, long-term, thought-provoking, time-consuming, eye-catching, record-breaking, self-aggrandizement, all-encompassing, muscle-bound, cost-effective, widespread, heartfelt, ongoing, so-called, phrase, informative
intro:
<h3>Más información en menos palabras</h3>
<p>El inglés escrito de nivel C2 condensa mucha información en el sintagma nominal, delante y detrás del núcleo.</p>
<div class="example">an apology that was long overdue and carefully worded<br>→ <strong>a long-overdue, carefully worded apology</strong></div>
<h3>Delante del nombre</h3>
<table><tr><td>adjetivos compuestos</td><td>a <strong>far-reaching</strong> reform · a <strong>thought-provoking</strong> film · a <strong>short-lived</strong> government</td></tr>
<tr><td>número + nombre en singular</td><td>a <strong>ten-minute</strong> walk · a <strong>five-year-old</strong> girl (✗ <i>five-years-old</i>)</td></tr>
<tr><td>nombre + nombre</td><td>a <strong>government health</strong> campaign · <strong>customer satisfaction</strong> levels</td></tr></table>
<h3>Detrás del nombre</h3>
<div class="example">the woman <strong>in charge of the project</strong> (preposición)<br>the issues <strong>raised at the meeting</strong> (participio)<br>the person <strong>to ask</strong> (infinitivo)<br>Mary Beard, <strong>the classicist</strong>, … (aposición)</div>
<div class="tip">Para traducir del español, busca los «de» encadenados: <i>la campaña de salud del gobierno</i> → <b>the government health campaign</b>.</div>
<div class="warning">Los números en adjetivos compuestos van en singular y con guiones: <i>a <b>three-day</b> conference</i>, no <i>a three-days conference</i>.</div>
ex:
fill ¦ It was a ___ walk to the station. ¦ ten-minute ¦ ten-minutes / ten minutes' long / ten minute's ¦ Era un paseo de diez minutos hasta la estación. ¦ Número + nombre en singular con guion.
fill ¦ The reform will have ___ consequences for the economy. ¦ far-reaching ¦ far-reached / far-reach / reaching-far ¦ La reforma tendrá consecuencias de gran alcance para la economía. ¦ far-reaching = de gran alcance.
fill ¦ The new party was ___ ; it disappeared within a year. ¦ short-lived ¦ short-living / life-short / short-life ¦ El nuevo partido duró poco; desapareció en menos de un año. ¦ short-lived = efímero.
fill ¦ She's the person ___ if you have any questions. ¦ to ask ¦ asking / asked / for asking ¦ Es la persona a la que hay que preguntar si tienes dudas. ¦ Posmodificación con infinitivo.
choose ¦ «la campaña de seguridad vial del ayuntamiento» ¦ the council's road safety campaign ¦ the campaign of safety of road of the council / the council road's safety campaign / the safety road council campaign
choose ¦ Elige la frase correcta. ¦ They have a six-year-old son. ¦ They have a six-years-old son. / They have a six-year-olds son. / They have a son six-years-old.
order ¦ The issues raised at the meeting will be discussed next week. ¦ Los temas planteados en la reunión se tratarán la semana que viene.
order ¦ It was a thought-provoking and beautifully shot documentary. ¦ Fue un documental que daba que pensar y estaba muy bien rodado.
translate ¦ una conferencia de tres días ¦ a three-day conference ¦ a three-day congress
translate ¦ un problema muy arraigado ¦ a deep-rooted problem ¦ a deep-seated problem / a deeply rooted problem / a long-standing problem
match ¦ overdue=atrasado, muy esperado; ill-advised=desacertado; hard-won=conseguido con esfuerzo; well-founded=fundado; wide-ranging=amplio, variado

=== 6
title: He purports to be an expert ¦ Dice ser un experto
desc: Verbos de habla y de opinión con matices: distanciamiento, valoración y estilo indirecto avanzado
gram: reporting verbs with complex patterns (purport to, allege that, concede that, dismiss sth as) ¦ reported speech without backshift ¦ journalistic distancing (reportedly, allegedly, it has been claimed that)
ref: c2-reporting
words: purport, allege, allegedly, concede, contend, assert, maintain, insinuate, imply, infer, dismiss, refute, rebut, disclose, divulge, reiterate, retract, acknowledge, intimate, hint, denounce, rebuke, disparage, laud, extol, decry, informant, testimony, hearsay, verbatim
intro:
<h3>Verbos de habla con matiz</h3>
<table><tr><td><strong>purport to be / to have</strong></td><td>a letter <strong>purporting to be</strong> from the king (que pretende ser)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>concede that</strong></td><td>She <strong>conceded that</strong> mistakes had been made. (admitir a regañadientes)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>contend / maintain that</strong></td><td>He <strong>maintains that</strong> he is innocent. (sostener)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>dismiss sth as</strong></td><td>They <strong>dismissed</strong> the report <strong>as</strong> nonsense. (descartar como)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>refute / rebut</strong></td><td>to <strong>refute</strong> an argument (rebatir con pruebas)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>reiterate / retract</strong></td><td>She <strong>reiterated</strong> her offer. · He <strong>retracted</strong> his statement.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>insinuate / intimate / hint</strong></td><td>Are you <strong>insinuating</strong> that I lied?</td></tr></table>
<h3>Estilo indirecto: ¿siempre se cambian los tiempos?</h3>
<p>No. Si lo dicho <b>sigue siendo verdad</b> o el verbo introductor está en presente, el tiempo puede mantenerse: <i>She told me she <b>lives</b> in Leeds</i> (y sigue viviendo allí).</p>
<h3>Distanciamiento periodístico</h3>
<div class="example">The minister <strong>allegedly</strong> accepted the money. · <strong>It has been claimed that</strong>… · <strong>According to</strong> unnamed sources… · The company <strong>is reported to have</strong> lost millions.</div>
<div class="warning"><b>imply</b> lo hace el hablante (insinuar); <b>infer</b> lo hace el oyente (deducir): <i>What are you <b>implying</b>? — I <b>inferred</b> from his tone that he was angry.</i></div>
ex:
fill ¦ The document ___ to be a copy of the original contract. ¦ purports ¦ pretends / claims of / alleges ¦ El documento pretende ser una copia del contrato original. ¦ purport to be.
fill ¦ He reluctantly ___ that he had been wrong. ¦ conceded ¦ refuted / dismissed / insinuated ¦ Admitió a regañadientes que se había equivocado. ¦ concede = admitir (a disgusto).
fill ¦ The critics dismissed the novel ___ sentimental rubbish. ¦ as ¦ like / for / of ¦ Los críticos despacharon la novela como basura sentimental. ¦ dismiss sth as.
fill ¦ From her silence, I ___ that she disagreed. ¦ inferred ¦ implied / insinuated / alleged ¦ Por su silencio, deduje que no estaba de acuerdo. ¦ infer = deducir (el oyente).
choose ¦ «Al parecer, el alcalde aceptó sobornos.» (periodístico) ¦ The mayor allegedly accepted bribes. ¦ The mayor alleged accepting bribes. / The mayor was alleging bribes. / Allegedly the mayor alleged bribes.
choose ¦ Retirar lo dicho es… ¦ retract ¦ reiterate / divulge / extol
order ¦ She maintains that she knew nothing about the deal. ¦ Sostiene que no sabía nada del acuerdo.
order ¦ The company is reported to have lost millions. ¦ Se informa de que la empresa ha perdido millones.
translate ¦ ¿Estás insinuando que miento? ¦ Are you implying that I'm lying? ¦ Are you insinuating that I'm lying? / Are you implying I'm lying? / Are you insinuating I'm lying?
translate ¦ Reiteró su oferta. ¦ She reiterated her offer. ¦ He reiterated his offer.
match ¦ divulge=revelar; extol=ensalzar; decry=censurar; hearsay=rumores; verbatim=palabra por palabra

=== 7
title: In the light of recent events ¦ A la luz de los acontecimientos recientes
desc: Locuciones preposicionales y preposiciones dependientes de nivel avanzado
gram: locuciones formales (in the light of, by virtue of, in lieu of, on the verge of, with a view to) ¦ preposiciones dependientes (averse to, conducive to, commensurate with, tantamount to) ¦ preposition + -ing
ref: c2-prepositions
words: light, virtue, lieu, verge, brink, view, behalf, regard, respect, accordance, conjunction, pursuit, averse, conducive, commensurate, tantamount, attuned, prone, susceptible, akin, subject, oblivious, adjacent, pertaining, vis-a-vis, amid, notwithstanding, pending, barring, save
intro:
<h3>Locuciones preposicionales formales</h3>
<table><tr><td><strong>in (the) light of</strong></td><td>a la luz de</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>by virtue of</strong></td><td>en virtud de</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>in lieu of</strong></td><td>en lugar de</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>on the verge / brink of</strong></td><td>al borde de</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>with a view to</strong> + -ing</td><td>con vistas a</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>on behalf of</strong></td><td>en nombre de</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>in accordance with</strong></td><td>de conformidad con</td></tr>

<div class="tip">Fíjate en el acento: en el verbo va en la partícula (<i>to hold <b>UP</b></i>), en el sustantivo va en la primera parte (<i>a <b>HOLD</b>-up</i>).</div>
<div class="warning">Son muy coloquiales. En un informe formal, <i>the talks petered out</i> puede sustituirse por <i>the talks gradually came to an end</i>.</div>

<p>El inglés literario, publicitario y periodístico juega con el sonido. La <b>aliteración</b> (repetir la consonante inicial) es especialmente frecuente:</p>
<div class="example"><strong>b</strong>ig <strong>b</strong>usiness · <strong>f</strong>ast and <strong>f</strong>urious · <strong>s</strong>ilent <strong>s</strong>treets · <strong>P</strong>ride and <strong>P</strong>rejudice (1813)</div>

<h3>Lenguaje arcaico y elevado</h3>
<div class="example"><strong>hitherto</strong> (hasta ahora) · <strong>whence</strong> (de donde) · <strong>ere</strong> (antes de) · <strong>thus</strong> (así) · <strong>verily</strong> (en verdad) · <strong>thou / thee / thy</strong> (tú / te / tu, en textos antiguos)</div>
<div class="tip">En el análisis de textos de C2 no basta con entender: hay que explicar <b>qué efecto</b> produce un recurso (ritmo, solemnidad, ironía, humor).</div>
<div class="warning">Los arcaísmos en un texto moderno suelen tener intención irónica o solemne. No los uses en tus redacciones salvo con ese fin.</div>
