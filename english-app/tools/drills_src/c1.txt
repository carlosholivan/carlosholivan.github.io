## inversion | 🔁 | Inversion | La inversión | never have I, not only did, no sooner had, little did, under no circumstances
Never ___ I been so embarrassed in my life. ¦ have ¦ I have / did / was ¦ Nunca en mi vida había pasado tanta vergüenza. ¦ Never + auxiliar + sujeto.
Rarely ___ he admit he is wrong. ¦ does ¦ he does / is / has ¦ Rara vez admite que se equivoca. ¦ Presente simple → does + sujeto.
Not only ___ she win the race, but she also broke the record. ¦ did ¦ she did / had / was ¦ No solo ganó la carrera, sino que batió el récord. ¦ Not only + did + sujeto.
No sooner had I sat down ___ the doorbell rang. ¦ than ¦ when / that / then ¦ Nada más sentarme, sonó el timbre. ¦ No sooner … than.
Hardly had we started eating ___ the lights went out. ¦ when ¦ than / that / then ¦ Apenas habíamos empezado a comer cuando se fue la luz. ¦ Hardly … when.
Little ___ they suspect that the house was haunted. ¦ did ¦ were / had / do ¦ Poco sospechaban ellos que la casa estaba encantada. ¦ Little did + sujeto + infinitivo.
Under no circumstances ___ the door be left open. ¦ should ¦ ought / will to / must not ¦ Bajo ninguna circunstancia debe dejarse la puerta abierta. ¦ Under no circumstances + should.
Only after the film had finished ___ I realise who the killer was. ¦ did ¦ had / I did / was ¦ Solo cuando terminó la película me di cuenta de quién era el asesino. ¦ La inversión va en la oración principal.
Not until the next morning ___ we hear the news. ¦ did ¦ we did / had / do ¦ Hasta la mañana siguiente no supimos la noticia. ¦ Not until … + did + sujeto.
On no ___ should you sign without reading the contract. ¦ account ¦ way / case / reason ¦ Bajo ningún concepto debes firmar sin leer el contrato. ¦ On no account = bajo ningún concepto.
## cleft | 🎯 | Cleft sentences and emphasis | Oraciones escindidas y énfasis | What I need is, It was … who, All I want, do enfático
___ I need is a long holiday. ¦ What ¦ That / Which / All what ¦ Lo que necesito son unas largas vacaciones. ¦ What … is.
It was my grandmother ___ taught me to cook. ¦ who ¦ which / what / whose ¦ Fue mi abuela quien me enseñó a cocinar. ¦ It was + persona + who.
All I want ___ to be left alone. ¦ is ¦ it is / are / that ¦ Lo único que quiero es que me dejen en paz. ¦ All (that) … is.
What she did ___ phone the police. ¦ was ¦ did / was to be / it was ¦ Lo que hizo fue llamar a la policía. ¦ What … did was + infinitivo.
The reason ___ I'm late is the traffic. ¦ why ¦ because / for / of ¦ La razón por la que llego tarde es el tráfico. ¦ The reason why…
It was in 2010 ___ they first met. ¦ that ¦ when it / which / where ¦ Fue en 2010 cuando se conocieron. ¦ It was + tiempo + that.
I ___ like your new haircut! It really suits you. ¦ do ¦ am / does / did be ¦ ¡Me encanta de verdad tu nuevo corte de pelo! Te queda genial. ¦ do enfático.
It was ___ an interesting talk that nobody left early. ¦ such ¦ so / too / very ¦ Fue una charla tan interesante que nadie se fue antes. ¦ such a + adj. + sust.
___ you do, don't tell him I told you. ¦ Whatever ¦ However / Whichever way / What ¦ Hagas lo que hagas, no le digas que te lo conté yo. ¦ Whatever = lo que sea que.
___ hard I study, I always forget the dates. ¦ However ¦ Whatever / Much / So ¦ Por mucho que estudie, siempre me olvido de las fechas. ¦ However + adj./adv.
## modals | 🧠 | Advanced modals | Modales avanzados | needn't have, bound to, may well, might as well, was to have
You ___ have brought a present. It wasn't necessary. ¦ needn't ¦ didn't need / mustn't / couldn't ¦ No hacía falta que trajeras un regalo. No era necesario. ¦ Lo trajo, pero no hacía falta.
I ___ to wear a suit, so I went in jeans. ¦ didn't need ¦ needn't have / mustn't / needn't ¦ No hacía falta que me pusiera traje, así que fui en vaqueros. ¦ No era necesario y no lo hizo.
They're bound ___ notice the mistake. ¦ to ¦ for / that / of ¦ Seguro que se dan cuenta del error. ¦ be bound to + infinitivo.
She may ___ be right about the dates. ¦ well ¦ good / as well / much ¦ Es muy posible que tenga razón con las fechas. ¦ may well = es muy posible.
The museum is closed. We might as ___ go for a coffee. ¦ well ¦ soon / good / long ¦ El museo está cerrado. Ya puestos, vamos a tomar un café. ¦ might as well.
He ___ have stolen it; he was with me all evening. ¦ can't ¦ mustn't / shouldn't / needn't ¦ No puede haberlo robado; estuvo conmigo toda la tarde. ¦ can't have = imposible.
We were ___ have flown to Rome, but the flight was cancelled. ¦ to ¦ going / due / about ¦ Íbamos a volar a Roma, pero cancelaron el vuelo. ¦ was/were to have + participio.
It's surprising that she ___ say such a thing. ¦ should ¦ would to / must / shall ¦ Es sorprendente que diga algo así. ¦ should de sorpresa.
You ___ have told me earlier! Now it's too late. ¦ should ¦ must / would / can ¦ ¡Me lo deberías haber dicho antes! Ahora es demasiado tarde. ¦ should have = crítica.
He ___ have missed the train; he's never late. ¦ must ¦ should / needn't / can ¦ Debe de haber perdido el tren; nunca llega tarde. ¦ must have = deducción.
## conditionals | 🔀 | Advanced conditionals and wishes | Condicionales avanzadas y deseos | had I known, should you, but for, provided, wish, would rather
___ I known you were coming, I'd have cooked. ¦ Had ¦ If / Should / Were ¦ Si hubiera sabido que venías, habría cocinado. ¦ Had + sujeto + participio.
___ you require assistance, please ask at reception. ¦ Should ¦ Would / Had / Did ¦ Si necesita ayuda, pregunte en recepción. ¦ Should + sujeto (formal).
___ I in your shoes, I'd accept the offer. ¦ Were ¦ Was / Had / Am ¦ Si yo estuviera en tu lugar, aceptaría la oferta. ¦ Were + sujeto.
But ___ the storm, we would have arrived on time. ¦ for ¦ of / with / that ¦ De no ser por la tormenta, habríamos llegado a tiempo. ¦ But for + sustantivo.
You can come ___ you don't make any noise. ¦ provided ¦ unless / otherwise / despite ¦ Puedes venir siempre que no hagas ruido. ¦ provided (that).
Take an umbrella, ___ you'll get soaked. ¦ otherwise ¦ unless / provided / in case of ¦ Coge un paraguas; si no, te vas a empapar. ¦ otherwise = si no.
I'd rather you ___ smoke in the house. ¦ didn't ¦ don't / won't / not ¦ Preferiría que no fumaras en casa. ¦ would rather + sujeto + pasado.
It's about time you ___ a proper job. ¦ found ¦ find / will find / have found ¦ Ya va siendo hora de que encuentres un trabajo de verdad. ¦ It's time + pasado.
If only I ___ that email! ¦ hadn't sent ¦ didn't send / wouldn't send / haven't sent ¦ ¡Ojalá no hubiera enviado ese correo! ¦ Lamento pasado → pasado perfecto.
He acts as though he ___ the owner. ¦ were ¦ is being / would be / be ¦ Se comporta como si fuera el dueño. ¦ as though + pasado.
## passive | 📰 | Passive and causatives | Pasiva y causativas | have sth done, get sb to, is said to have, needs doing
We had the windows ___ last week. ¦ cleaned ¦ clean / to clean / cleaning ¦ Nos limpiaron las ventanas la semana pasada. ¦ have + objeto + participio.
I'll get my brother ___ me move the sofa. ¦ to help ¦ help / helped / helping ¦ Conseguiré que mi hermano me ayude a mover el sofá. ¦ get + persona + to.
I'll have the porter ___ your bags up. ¦ bring ¦ to bring / brought / bringing ¦ Haré que el portero le suba las maletas. ¦ have + persona + infinitivo sin to.
She had her phone ___ on holiday. ¦ stolen ¦ steal / stole / stealing ¦ Le robaron el móvil en las vacaciones. ¦ have sth done (algo malo).
The suspect is believed ___ the country. ¦ to have left ¦ to leave / having left / that he left ¦ Se cree que el sospechoso ha salido del país. ¦ Pasado → to have + participio.
It ___ that the company will close two factories. ¦ is reported ¦ reports / is reporting / has reported it ¦ Se informa de que la empresa cerrará dos fábricas. ¦ It is reported that…
The car needs ___ . ¦ servicing ¦ service / to service / serviced ¦ Al coche le hace falta una revisión. ¦ need + -ing (pasiva).
I don't like ___ what to do. ¦ being told ¦ telling / be told / to tell ¦ No me gusta que me digan lo que tengo que hacer. ¦ Gerundio pasivo.
She expects ___ promoted next year. ¦ to be ¦ being / be / to been ¦ Espera que la asciendan el año que viene. ¦ Infinitivo pasivo.
The bridge is thought ___ in Roman times. ¦ to have been built ¦ to be built / to build / having built ¦ Se cree que el puente se construyó en época romana. ¦ Infinitivo perfecto pasivo.
## linkers | 🔗 | Advanced linkers | Conectores avanzados | albeit, whereas, much as, hence, owing to, irrespective of
It was a successful, ___ expensive, project. ¦ albeit ¦ although / despite / whereas ¦ Fue un proyecto exitoso, aunque caro. ¦ albeit + adjetivo.
I love the sea, ___ my husband prefers the mountains. ¦ whereas ¦ albeit / despite / however ¦ A mí me encanta el mar, mientras que mi marido prefiere la montaña. ¦ whereas = mientras que.
___ as I like her, I can't lend her any more money. ¦ Much ¦ So / As / How ¦ Por mucho que me caiga bien, no puedo prestarle más dinero. ¦ Much as.
The match was postponed ___ to the snow. ¦ owing ¦ because / since / given ¦ El partido se aplazó debido a la nieve. ¦ owing to + sustantivo.
Sales fell by 20%; ___ the job cuts. ¦ hence ¦ whereas / albeit / despite ¦ Las ventas cayeron un 20 %; de ahí los despidos. ¦ hence = de ahí.
Everyone is welcome, ___ of age. ¦ irrespective ¦ despite / regardless to / instead ¦ Todo el mundo es bienvenido, independientemente de la edad. ¦ irrespective of.
___ that it's raining, we'll eat inside. ¦ Given ¦ Owing / Despite / Although ¦ Dado que llueve, comeremos dentro. ¦ Given that = dado que.
Strange ___ it may seem, I enjoy doing housework. ¦ as ¦ though it / so / how ¦ Por extraño que parezca, disfruto haciendo las tareas de casa. ¦ Adj. + as + sujeto + verbo.
The plan is risky. ___ , it's our only option. ¦ Nevertheless ¦ Whereas / Albeit / Hence ¦ El plan es arriesgado. Aun así, es nuestra única opción. ¦ Nevertheless = sin embargo.
___ , the hotel was a bit noisy, but the location was perfect. ¦ Admittedly ¦ Consequently / Hence / Whereas ¦ Es cierto que el hotel era algo ruidoso, pero la ubicación era perfecta. ¦ Admittedly = hay que reconocer que.
## phrasal | 🧲 | Phrasal verbs C1 | Phrasal verbs C1 | rule out, iron out, play down, live up to, brush up on
The police have ruled ___ the possibility of murder. ¦ out ¦ off / over / down ¦ La policía ha descartado la posibilidad de un asesinato. ¦ rule out = descartar.
We need to iron ___ a few details before signing. ¦ out ¦ off / up / down ¦ Tenemos que limar algunos detalles antes de firmar. ¦ iron out.
The minister played ___ the risks. ¦ down ¦ off / out / up ¦ El ministro quitó importancia a los riesgos. ¦ play down.
The hotel didn't live up ___ the photos on its website. ¦ to ¦ with / for / at ¦ El hotel no estuvo a la altura de las fotos de su web. ¦ live up to.
I must brush up ___ my German before the trip. ¦ on ¦ with / at / for ¦ Tengo que repasar mi alemán antes del viaje. ¦ brush up on.
The old notes are being phased ___ . ¦ out ¦ off / down / away ¦ Los billetes antiguos se están retirando poco a poco. ¦ phase out.
After the scandal, the director stepped ___ . ¦ down ¦ off / out / over ¦ Tras el escándalo, el director dimitió. ¦ step down.
I can't put up ___ his rudeness any longer. ¦ with ¦ to / on / for ¦ No aguanto más su mala educación. ¦ put up with.
The idea never really caught ___ . ¦ on ¦ up / in / off ¦ La idea nunca llegó a cuajar. ¦ catch on = ponerse de moda.
Don't bring ___ politics at dinner, please. ¦ up ¦ out / in / over ¦ No saques el tema de la política en la cena, por favor. ¦ bring up = sacar un tema.
## words | 🔤 | Word formation, collocations and idioms | Formación de palabras, colocaciones y modismos | mis-, out-, -en, safe and sound, a piece of cake
Don't ___ the difficulty of the exam. ¦ underestimate ¦ overestimate / misestimate / outestimate ¦ No subestimes la dificultad del examen. ¦ under- = por debajo.
His comments were completely ___ . ¦ misleading ¦ misled / mislead / unleading ¦ Sus comentarios eran totalmente engañosos. ¦ mis- + leading.
The road will be ___ next year. ¦ widened ¦ widen / width / wided ¦ Ensancharán la carretera el año que viene. ¦ wide → widen.
The ___ of rain tomorrow is low. ¦ likelihood ¦ likely / likeness / likelihoods ¦ La probabilidad de lluvia mañana es baja. ¦ likely → likelihood.
After the storm, the climbers returned safe and ___ . ¦ sound ¦ well / safe / healthy ¦ Tras la tormenta, los escaladores volvieron sanos y salvos. ¦ safe and sound.
I'm sick and ___ of your excuses. ¦ tired ¦ fed / bored / ill ¦ Estoy harto de tus excusas. ¦ sick and tired.
It's ___ unlikely that we'll win. ¦ highly ¦ strongly / heavily / deeply ¦ Es muy poco probable que ganemos. ¦ highly unlikely.
Don't beat about the ___ . Just tell me. ¦ bush ¦ tree / house / hedge ¦ No te andes con rodeos. Dímelo. ¦ beat about the bush.

He takes his parents' help for ___ . ¦ granted ¦ given / sure / certain ¦ Da por sentada la ayuda de sus padres. ¦ take for granted.
## register | 🎩 | Register and hedging | Registro y lenguaje tentativo | obtain, prior to, failure to, appear to, arguably, to some extent
Visitors must ___ a pass at reception. ¦ obtain ¦ get to / commence / endeavour ¦ Los visitantes deben obtener un pase en recepción. ¦ obtain = get (formal).
Please arrive 30 minutes ___ to departure. ¦ prior ¦ before / previous / ahead ¦ Por favor, preséntese 30 minutos antes de la salida. ¦ prior to = before.
___ to pay on time will result in a fine. ¦ Failure ¦ Fail / Failing of / Failed ¦ No pagar a tiempo supondrá una multa. ¦ Failure to + infinitivo.
The ceremony will ___ at 10 a.m. ¦ commence ¦ obtain / endeavour / facilitate ¦ La ceremonia comenzará a las 10. ¦ commence = start.
The results ___ to support the theory. ¦ appear ¦ look / show / are seemed ¦ Los resultados parecen respaldar la teoría. ¦ appear to + infinitivo.
She is ___ the best player of her generation. ¦ arguably ¦ arguing / argued / argumentative ¦ Se podría decir que es la mejor jugadora de su generación. ¦ arguably.
I agree with you to some ___ . ¦ extent ¦ part / grade / way ¦ Estoy de acuerdo contigo hasta cierto punto. ¦ to some extent.
It could be ___ that the law is unfair. ¦ argued ¦ arguing / argue / arguably ¦ Se podría argumentar que la ley es injusta. ¦ It could be argued that…
There were thirty ___ so guests. ¦ or ¦ and / about / more ¦ Había unos treinta invitados. ¦ or so = aproximadamente.
We will ___ to reply within 48 hours. ¦ endeavour ¦ obtain / commence / comply ¦ Procuraremos responder en un plazo de 48 horas. ¦ endeavour = try (formal).
