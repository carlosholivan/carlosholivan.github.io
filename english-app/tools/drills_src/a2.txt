## past | 📜 | Past simple and past continuous | Pasado simple y continuo | historias, when / while, irregulares
I ___ a shower when the phone rang. ¦ was having ¦ had / have / was have ¦ Me estaba duchando cuando sonó el teléfono. ¦ Acción larga interrumpida → pasado continuo.
While we were walking, it ___ to rain. ¦ started ¦ was starting / starts / start ¦ Mientras paseábamos, empezó a llover. ¦ Acción corta → pasado simple.
She ___ her keys yesterday. ¦ lost ¦ losed / lose / was losing ¦ Ayer perdió las llaves. ¦ lose → lost.
What ___ you doing at nine last night? ¦ were ¦ did / was / are ¦ ¿Qué estabas haciendo anoche a las nueve? ¦ you → were + -ing.
We ___ to the party because we were tired. ¦ didn't go ¦ didn't went / weren't go / not went ¦ No fuimos a la fiesta porque estábamos cansados. ¦ didn't + forma base.
When I arrived, the guests ___ . ¦ were dancing ¦ danced / was dancing / dance ¦ Cuando llegué, los invitados estaban bailando. ¦ Escena de fondo → pasado continuo.
He ___ his leg when he was skiing. ¦ broke ¦ was breaking / breaked / broken ¦ Se rompió la pierna cuando esquiaba. ¦ break → broke.
___ you see the news last night? ¦ Did ¦ Were / Have / Do ¦ ¿Viste las noticias anoche? ¦ Pregunta en pasado simple → Did.
I ___ him while I was waiting for the bus. ¦ saw ¦ was seeing / see / seen ¦ Lo vi mientras esperaba el autobús. ¦ see no va en continuo.
They ___ married in 2010. ¦ got ¦ get / were get / have got ¦ Se casaron en 2010. ¦ get married → got married.
## comparing | ⚖️ | Comparatives and superlatives | Comparativos y superlativos | -er, more, the most, as…as
My flat is ___ than yours. ¦ bigger ¦ more big / biger / the biggest ¦ Mi piso es más grande que el tuyo. ¦ big → bigger.
This is the ___ book I've ever read. ¦ best ¦ better / goodest / most good ¦ Es el mejor libro que he leído nunca. ¦ good → better → the best.
Spanish is ___ than English for me. ¦ easier ¦ more easy / easyer / easiest ¦ Para mí el español es más fácil que el inglés. ¦ -y → -ier.
She's as tall ___ her father. ¦ as ¦ than / like / that ¦ Es tan alta como su padre. ¦ as … as.
It's the most expensive restaurant ___ the city. ¦ in ¦ of / at / from ¦ Es el restaurante más caro de la ciudad. ¦ Lugar → in.
Today is much ___ than yesterday. ¦ colder ¦ more cold / coldest / cold ¦ Hoy hace mucho más frío que ayer. ¦ much + comparativo.
My cold is ___ today. I feel terrible. ¦ worse ¦ worst / badder / more bad ¦ Mi resfriado está peor hoy. ¦ bad → worse → the worst.
The train is ___ than the bus. ¦ more comfortable ¦ comfortabler / most comfortable / more comfortabler ¦ El tren es más cómodo que el autobús. ¦ Adjetivo largo → more.
It was the ___ day of the year. ¦ hottest ¦ hotest / most hot / hotter ¦ Fue el día más caluroso del año. ¦ hot → hottest (doble t).
This village isn't ___ noisy as the city. ¦ as ¦ so much / than / more ¦ Este pueblo no es tan ruidoso como la ciudad. ¦ not as … as.
## modals | 🚦 | Modals: must, have to, should | Modales: obligación y consejo | must, mustn't, (don't) have to, should
You ___ smoke in the hospital. ¦ mustn't ¦ don't have to / haven't to / not must ¦ No se puede fumar en el hospital. ¦ Prohibición → mustn't.
I ___ wear a uniform at my school. ¦ have to ¦ has to / must to / am have to ¦ En mi colegio tengo que llevar uniforme. ¦ I → have to.
You ___ come if you don't want to. ¦ don't have to ¦ mustn't / haven't to / doesn't have to ¦ No tienes que venir si no quieres. ¦ No es necesario → don't have to.
You look ill. You ___ see a doctor. ¦ should ¦ should to / musts / have ¦ Tienes mala cara. Deberías ir al médico. ¦ Consejo → should.
Yesterday I ___ work until nine. ¦ had to ¦ must / have to / musted ¦ Ayer tuve que trabajar hasta las nueve. ¦ Pasado → had to.
___ she have to work on Saturdays? ¦ Does ¦ Has / Do / Must ¦ ¿Tiene que trabajar los sábados? ¦ Pregunta con have to → Does she have to…?
You ___ eat so much sugar. ¦ shouldn't ¦ don't should / mustn't to / not should ¦ No deberías comer tanto azúcar. ¦ Consejo negativo → shouldn't.
Children ___ pay. It's free for them. ¦ don't have to ¦ mustn't / don't must / haven't ¦ Los niños no pagan. Es gratis para ellos. ¦ No es necesario → don't have to.
Passengers ___ show their tickets. ¦ must ¦ must to / musts / have ¦ Los pasajeros deben enseñar el billete. ¦ Norma escrita → must.
___ I take an umbrella? ¦ Should ¦ Must to / Do I should / Have ¦ ¿Me llevo paraguas? ¦ Pedir consejo → Should I…?
## future | 🔮 | will, going to, present continuous | El futuro | predicciones, planes y decisiones
— I'm hungry. — I ___ make you a sandwich. ¦ 'll ¦ 'm going to / will to / make ¦ —Tengo hambre. —Te hago un bocadillo. ¦ Decisión en el momento → will.
We ___ to Paris next week. The tickets are booked. ¦ 're flying ¦ fly / will fly / flew ¦ La semana que viene volamos a París. Ya tenemos los billetes. ¦ Plan fijado → presente continuo.
Look at the sky! It ___ rain. ¦ 's going to ¦ will / rains / 's raining to ¦ ¡Mira el cielo! Va a llover. ¦ Predicción con pruebas → going to.
I think it ___ be a great party. ¦ will ¦ is / going / would to ¦ Creo que será una fiesta genial. ¦ Opinión sobre el futuro → will.
I promise I ___ tell anyone. ¦ won't ¦ don't / willn't / am not ¦ Prometo que no se lo diré a nadie. ¦ Promesa → won't.
What ___ you going to do after university? ¦ are ¦ will / do / did ¦ ¿Qué vas a hacer después de la universidad? ¦ be going to.
I don't think she ___ come. ¦ 'll ¦ won't / is / comes ¦ No creo que venga. ¦ I don't think + will.
She ___ probably be late. ¦ 'll ¦ is / would / does ¦ Probablemente llegará tarde. ¦ will + probably.
— Shall I help you? — Thanks, that ___ great. ¦ would be ¦ will to be / is being / be ¦ —¿Te ayudo? —Gracias, sería genial. ¦ would be = sería.
My brother ___ married in June. ¦ is getting ¦ gets / will get to / getting ¦ Mi hermano se casa en junio. ¦ Plan fijado → presente continuo.
## perfect | ✅ | Present perfect | Present perfect | experiencias, just / already / yet, for / since
Have you ever ___ to New York? ¦ been ¦ gone / went / be ¦ ¿Has estado alguna vez en Nueva York? ¦ Experiencia → been to.
I've ___ finished. Here you are. ¦ just ¦ yet / since / ever ¦ Acabo de terminar. Aquí tienes. ¦ just = acabar de.
Has the film started ___ ? ¦ yet ¦ already / just / still ¦ ¿Ya ha empezado la película? ¦ yet al final de la pregunta.
She ___ never flown in a plane. ¦ has ¦ have / is / did ¦ Nunca ha volado en avión. ¦ she → has.
I ___ that film last week. ¦ saw ¦ have seen / 've saw / seen ¦ Vi esa película la semana pasada. ¦ last week → pasado simple.
We've lived here ___ ten years. ¦ for ¦ since / during / from ¦ Vivimos aquí desde hace diez años. ¦ for + periodo.
I've known him ___ 2015. ¦ since ¦ for / from / during ¦ Lo conozco desde 2015. ¦ since + punto de inicio.
They've ___ eaten. They aren't hungry. ¦ already ¦ yet / ever / still ¦ Ya han comido. No tienen hambre. ¦ already = ya.
Tom isn't here. He's ___ to the shop. ¦ gone ¦ been / went / go ¦ Tom no está. Ha ido a la tienda. ¦ gone = se ha ido y no ha vuelto.
How many times ___ you been to London? ¦ have ¦ did / are / were ¦ ¿Cuántas veces has estado en Londres? ¦ Experiencias → present perfect.
## patterns | 🧩 | Verb + to or -ing | Verbo + to o -ing | want to, enjoy -ing, would like to
I enjoy ___ to music. ¦ listening ¦ to listen / listen / listened ¦ Disfruto escuchando música. ¦ enjoy + -ing.
She wants ___ a doctor. ¦ to be ¦ be / being / to being ¦ Quiere ser médica. ¦ want + to.
Would you like ___ with us? ¦ to come ¦ coming / come / came ¦ ¿Te gustaría venir con nosotros? ¦ would like + to.
Have you finished ___ the report? ¦ writing ¦ to write / write / wrote ¦ ¿Has terminado de escribir el informe? ¦ finish + -ing.
I'm learning ___ the piano. ¦ to play ¦ playing / play / for play ¦ Estoy aprendiendo a tocar el piano. ¦ learn + to.
He's good at ___ . ¦ drawing ¦ to draw / draw / drawed ¦ Se le da bien dibujar. ¦ Tras preposición → -ing.
I went to the bank ___ some money. ¦ to get ¦ for get / for to get / getting ¦ Fui al banco a sacar dinero. ¦ Finalidad → to + verbo.
Don't forget ___ the door. ¦ to lock ¦ locking / lock / locked ¦ No olvides cerrar la puerta con llave. ¦ forget to = olvidarse de hacer.
They decided ___ a house. ¦ to buy ¦ buying / buy / for buying ¦ Decidieron comprar una casa. ¦ decide + to.
I'm looking forward to ___ you. ¦ seeing ¦ see / to see / saw ¦ Tengo ganas de verte. ¦ look forward to + -ing.
## quantity | 🧮 | Quantifiers and articles | Cuantificadores y artículos | a few, a little, too much, enough, the
There are too ___ people here. ¦ many ¦ much / enough / lot ¦ Aquí hay demasiada gente. ¦ people (contable) → many.
I've got a ___ friends in Madrid. ¦ few ¦ little / lot / many ¦ Tengo unos cuantos amigos en Madrid. ¦ Contable → a few.
Could I have a ___ milk in my tea? ¦ little ¦ few / lot / many ¦ ¿Me pones un poco de leche en el té? ¦ Incontable → a little.
This box isn't big ___ . ¦ enough ¦ too / much / so ¦ Esta caja no es lo bastante grande. ¦ adjetivo + enough.
I love ___ music. ¦ – ¦ the / a / an ¦ Me encanta la música. ¦ En general, sin artículo.
Can you pass me ___ salt, please? ¦ the ¦ a / – / some of ¦ ¿Me pasas la sal, por favor? ¦ Algo concreto que los dos vemos → the.
She plays ___ violin. ¦ the ¦ a / – / an ¦ Toca el violín. ¦ Instrumentos con the.
We had ___ lunch at one. ¦ – ¦ the / a / an ¦ Comimos a la una. ¦ Comidas sin artículo.
I drank too ___ coffee and couldn't sleep. ¦ much ¦ many / lot / enough ¦ Bebí demasiado café y no pude dormir. ¦ Incontable → much.
There's ___ of food. Help yourself! ¦ plenty ¦ many / enough / few ¦ Hay comida de sobra. ¡Sírvete! ¦ plenty of = de sobra.
## relative | 🔗 | Relative clauses and pronouns | Relativos y pronombres | who, which, where, whose, mine, myself
A pilot is a person ___ flies planes. ¦ who ¦ which / where / whose ¦ Un piloto es una persona que pilota aviones. ¦ Persona → who.
That's the shop ___ I bought my phone. ¦ where ¦ which / who / when ¦ Esa es la tienda donde compré el móvil. ¦ Lugar → where.
I've got a friend ___ brother is an actor. ¦ whose ¦ who / who's / which ¦ Tengo un amigo cuyo hermano es actor. ¦ Posesión → whose.
The book ___ you lent me is great. ¦ that ¦ who / where / what ¦ El libro que me prestaste es genial. ¦ Cosa → that / which.
Is this umbrella ___ ? ¦ yours ¦ your / your's / you ¦ ¿Este paraguas es tuyo? ¦ Pronombre posesivo → yours.
She's a friend of ___ . ¦ mine ¦ me / my / I ¦ Es una amiga mía. ¦ a friend of mine.
He cut ___ while he was cooking. ¦ himself ¦ him / hisself / itself ¦ Se cortó mientras cocinaba. ¦ Reflexivo de he → himself.
Help ___ to some cake! ¦ yourself ¦ you / yours / your ¦ ¡Sírvete pastel! ¦ help yourself = sírvete.
Our car is older than ___ . ¦ theirs ¦ their / them / their's ¦ Nuestro coche es más viejo que el suyo. ¦ Pronombre posesivo de they → theirs.
Do you know the man ___ is talking to Anna? ¦ who ¦ which / whose / where ¦ ¿Conoces al hombre que está hablando con Anna? ¦ Persona → who.
## conditional | 🔀 | First conditional and requests | Primer condicional y peticiones | if, unless, when, could, would
If it ___ tomorrow, we'll stay at home. ¦ rains ¦ will rain / rain / would rain ¦ Si llueve mañana, nos quedaremos en casa. ¦ if + presente.
If you hurry, you ___ the bus. ¦ 'll catch ¦ catch / caught / catching ¦ Si te das prisa, cogerás el autobús. ¦ Resultado → will.
I won't go ___ you come with me. ¦ unless ¦ if / when / except ¦ No iré a menos que vengas conmigo. ¦ unless = a menos que.
I'll call you when I ___ home. ¦ get ¦ will get / got / getting ¦ Te llamaré cuando llegue a casa. ¦ when + presente.
___ you help me with this box, please? ¦ Could ¦ Would mind / Do / Shall ¦ ¿Podrías ayudarme con esta caja? ¦ Petición educada → Could you…?
Would you mind ___ a bit quieter? ¦ being ¦ be / to be / been ¦ ¿Te importaría hacer menos ruido? ¦ mind + -ing.
I'd rather ___ at home tonight. ¦ stay ¦ to stay / staying / stayed ¦ Prefiero quedarme en casa esta noche. ¦ I'd rather + forma base.
___ you like a drink? ¦ Would ¦ Do / Will / Are ¦ ¿Quieres algo de beber? ¦ Ofrecer → Would you like…?
If you don't study, you ___ pass. ¦ won't ¦ don't / wouldn't / not ¦ Si no estudias, no aprobarás. ¦ Resultado negativo → won't.
What will you do if you ___ the exam? ¦ fail ¦ will fail / failed / failing ¦ ¿Qué harás si suspendes el examen? ¦ if + presente.
