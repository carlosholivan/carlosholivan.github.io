## be | 👋 | The verb to be | El verbo to be | am / is / are, negativas, preguntas y la edad
I ___ from Seville. ¦ am ¦ is / are / be ¦ Soy de Sevilla. ¦ Con I siempre am.
My parents ___ teachers. ¦ are ¦ is / am / be ¦ Mis padres son profesores. ¦ Sujeto plural (they) → are.
___ very cold today. ¦ It's ¦ It are / Is / It has ¦ Hoy hace mucho frío. ¦ El tiempo se expresa con it + be: it's cold.
She ___ 30 years old. ¦ is ¦ has / have / are ¦ Tiene 30 años. ¦ La edad va con be, no con have.
___ you a student? ¦ Are ¦ Is / Do / Am ¦ ¿Eres estudiante? ¦ En las preguntas be va delante del sujeto: Are you…?
We ___ tired. We're fine. ¦ aren't ¦ isn't / not / don't ¦ No estamos cansados. Estamos bien. ¦ we → are not → aren't.
Is your brother married? — No, he ___ . ¦ isn't ¦ not / doesn't / aren't ¦ ¿Tu hermano está casado? — No. ¦ Respuesta corta: No, he isn't.
Where ___ your keys? ¦ are ¦ is / am / be ¦ ¿Dónde están tus llaves? ¦ keys es plural → are.
Are they from China? — Yes, they ___ . ¦ are ¦ 're / is / do ¦ ¿Son de China? — Sí. ¦ En la respuesta corta afirmativa no se contrae: Yes, they are.
My name ___ Laura. ¦ is ¦ am / are / has ¦ Me llamo Laura. ¦ my name = it → is.
## present | ⏰ | Present simple | Presente simple | rutinas, -s de la 3.ª persona, do / does
She ___ in an office. ¦ works ¦ work / working / is work ¦ Trabaja en una oficina. ¦ he/she/it añade -s.
We ___ meat. We're vegetarian. ¦ don't eat ¦ doesn't eat / not eat / no eat ¦ No comemos carne. Somos vegetarianos. ¦ we → don't + verbo.
___ your sister like coffee? ¦ Does ¦ Do / Is / Has ¦ ¿A tu hermana le gusta el café? ¦ your sister = she → does.
He ___ TV every evening. ¦ watches ¦ watchs / watch / is watch ¦ Ve la tele todas las noches. ¦ -ch + es: watches.
My father ___ to work by bus. ¦ goes ¦ go / gos / is go ¦ Mi padre va a trabajar en autobús. ¦ go → goes.
What time ___ the shop open? ¦ does ¦ do / is / open ¦ ¿A qué hora abre la tienda? ¦ the shop = it → does.
Tom doesn't ___ breakfast. ¦ have ¦ has / having / to have ¦ Tom no desayuna. ¦ Tras doesn't, forma base sin -s.
She ___ English and French. ¦ studies ¦ studys / study / studyes ¦ Estudia inglés y francés. ¦ consonante + y → ies.
Do you live here? — Yes, I ___ . ¦ do ¦ live / am / does ¦ ¿Vives aquí? — Sí. ¦ Respuesta corta con do.
My brother ___ a car. ¦ has ¦ have / haves / is have ¦ Mi hermano tiene coche. ¦ have → has con he/she/it.
## things | 📦 | Articles, plurals and this / that | Artículos, plurales y demostrativos | a / an, plurales, this / these
I've got ___ idea! ¦ an ¦ a / – / some ¦ ¡Tengo una idea! ¦ idea empieza por sonido vocal → an.
She's ___ university student. ¦ a ¦ an / the / – ¦ Es estudiante universitaria. ¦ university suena /ju/ (consonante) → a.
I've got two ___ . ¦ children ¦ childs / childrens / child ¦ Tengo dos hijos. ¦ Plural irregular: child → children.
There are three ___ on the shelf. ¦ boxes ¦ boxs / box / boxies ¦ Hay tres cajas en la estantería. ¦ -x + es.
___ shoes are new. ¦ These ¦ This / That / It ¦ Estos zapatos son nuevos. ¦ Plural cercano → these.
Who is ___ man over there? ¦ that ¦ this / these / those ¦ ¿Quién es ese hombre de allí? ¦ Singular y lejos → that.
He's ___ engineer. ¦ an ¦ a / the / – ¦ Es ingeniero. ¦ Profesión con artículo; engineer empieza por vocal.
I need two ___ . ¦ new pens ¦ news pens / pens new / new pen ¦ Necesito dos bolis nuevos. ¦ El adjetivo va delante y no lleva -s.
How many ___ are there in your class? ¦ people ¦ peoples / persons / person ¦ ¿Cuántas personas hay en tu clase? ¦ Plural de person: people.
It's ___ hour from here. ¦ an ¦ a / – / one ¦ Está a una hora de aquí. ¦ La h de hour es muda → an.
## possessives | 🔑 | Possessives and pronouns | Posesivos y pronombres | my / his / her, 's, me / him / them
Anna lives with ___ brother. ¦ her ¦ his / their / she ¦ Anna vive con su hermano. ¦ La poseedora es Anna (ella) → her.
Peter and ___ wife are from York. ¦ his ¦ her / its / their ¦ Peter y su mujer son de York. ¦ El poseedor es Peter → his.
This is ___ car. ¦ my father's ¦ my fathers / the father of me / my father ¦ Este es el coche de mi padre. ¦ Genitivo sajón: my father's car.
Where's Tom? I can't see ___ . ¦ him ¦ he / his / them ¦ ¿Dónde está Tom? No lo veo. ¦ Objeto masculino singular → him.
These flowers are for ___ . ¦ you ¦ your / yours / you're ¦ Estas flores son para ti. ¦ Tras preposición, pronombre de objeto.
We love ___ grandparents. ¦ our ¦ us / ours / we ¦ Queremos a nuestros abuelos. ¦ Posesivo de we → our.
___ bag is this? — It's Maria's. ¦ Whose ¦ Who's / Who / Which ¦ ¿De quién es este bolso? — De María. ¦ ¿De quién? = whose (who's = who is).
The dog is eating ___ food. ¦ its ¦ it's / his / it ¦ El perro está comiendo su comida. ¦ Posesivo de it: its (sin apóstrofo).
Can you help ___ ? ¦ us ¦ we / our / ours ¦ ¿Nos puedes ayudar? ¦ Objeto de we → us.
My cousins live in Paris. I visit ___ every summer. ¦ them ¦ they / their / theirs ¦ Mis primos viven en París. Los visito cada verano. ¦ Objeto de they → them.
## can | 💪 | can and the imperative | can y el imperativo | can / can't, órdenes, let's
She can ___ the piano. ¦ play ¦ plays / to play / playing ¦ Sabe tocar el piano. ¦ can + forma base (sin to ni -s).
___ you swim? ¦ Can ¦ Do / Are / Cans ¦ ¿Sabes nadar? ¦ Pregunta con can: Can you…?
I ___ come to the party. I'm working. ¦ can't ¦ don't can / not can / can't to ¦ No puedo ir a la fiesta. Estoy trabajando. ¦ Negativa: can't.
___ the door, please. It's cold. ¦ Close ¦ You close / Closes / To close ¦ Cierra la puerta, por favor. Hace frío. ¦ Imperativo: forma base sin sujeto.
___ worry. It's OK. ¦ Don't ¦ No / Not / Doesn't ¦ No te preocupes. No pasa nada. ¦ Imperativo negativo: Don't + verbo.
___ go to the cinema tonight. ¦ Let's ¦ Lets / Let / We let ¦ Vamos al cine esta noche. ¦ Propuesta: Let's + verbo.
Can I use your phone? — Sorry, you ___ . ¦ can't ¦ don't / aren't / can ¦ ¿Puedo usar tu móvil? — Lo siento, no. ¦ Respuesta corta: No / Sorry, you can't.
My grandmother can't ___ a computer. ¦ use ¦ uses / to use / using ¦ Mi abuela no sabe usar un ordenador. ¦ can't + forma base.
___ careful! The floor is wet. ¦ Be ¦ Are / Is / You be ¦ ¡Ten cuidado! El suelo está mojado. ¦ Imperativo de be: Be careful.
He can speak French very ___ . ¦ well ¦ good / much / fine ¦ Habla francés muy bien. ¦ Adverbio: well (good es adjetivo).
## continuous | 🎬 | Present continuous or simple? | ¿Presente continuo o simple? | ahora (am / is / are + -ing) o siempre
Listen! The baby ___ . ¦ is crying ¦ cries / cry / crying ¦ ¡Escucha! El bebé está llorando. ¦ Listen! → algo que pasa ahora.
I usually ___ tea, but today I'm drinking coffee. ¦ drink ¦ am drinking / drinking / drinks ¦ Normalmente bebo té, pero hoy bebo café. ¦ usually → presente simple.
What ___ you doing now? ¦ are ¦ do / is / does ¦ ¿Qué estás haciendo ahora? ¦ now → presente continuo: are you doing.
They're ___ on the sofa. ¦ sitting ¦ siting / sit / sits ¦ Están sentados en el sofá. ¦ sit → sitting (se dobla la t).
It ___ a lot in winter here. ¦ rains ¦ is raining / raining / rain ¦ Aquí llueve mucho en invierno. ¦ Algo habitual → presente simple.
I ___ the answer. ¦ know ¦ am knowing / knowing / knows ¦ Sé la respuesta. ¦ know es un verbo de estado: no va en continuo.
She ___ a red dress today. ¦ is wearing ¦ wears / wear / is wear ¦ Hoy lleva un vestido rojo. ¦ today → continuo.
We ___ TV at the moment. ¦ aren't watching ¦ don't watch / not watching / isn't watching ¦ Ahora no estamos viendo la tele. ¦ at the moment → continuo; we → aren't.
Do you like jazz? — Yes, I ___ it. ¦ love ¦ am loving / loves / loving ¦ ¿Te gusta el jazz? — Sí, me encanta. ¦ love (gusto) no va en continuo.
He's ___ a letter to his grandmother. ¦ writing ¦ writeing / writting / write ¦ Está escribiendo una carta a su abuela. ¦ write → writing (se va la e).
## past | 📅 | Past: was / were and past simple | El pasado: was / were y pasado simple | ayer, el año pasado, did / didn't
I ___ at home yesterday. ¦ was ¦ were / am / did ¦ Ayer estuve en casa. ¦ I → was.
___ you at the party? ¦ Were ¦ Was / Did / Are ¦ ¿Estuviste en la fiesta? ¦ you → were.
We ___ to Rome last year. ¦ went ¦ go / goed / gone ¦ Fuimos a Roma el año pasado. ¦ go → went (irregular).
She ___ the film. ¦ didn't like ¦ didn't liked / not liked / doesn't liked ¦ No le gustó la película. ¦ didn't + forma base.
___ you see Tom yesterday? ¦ Did ¦ Do / Were / Was ¦ ¿Viste a Tom ayer? ¦ Pregunta en pasado: Did + sujeto + forma base.
They ___ in London for two years. ¦ lived ¦ live / lifed / living ¦ Vivieron en Londres dos años. ¦ live → lived (solo -d).
I ___ a new phone last week. ¦ bought ¦ buyed / buy / brought ¦ Compré un móvil nuevo la semana pasada. ¦ buy → bought (no confundir con brought, de bring).
He ___ born in 1995. ¦ was ¦ is / did / were ¦ Nació en 1995. ¦ be born: I was born.
We ___ the bus. We walked. ¦ didn't take ¦ didn't took / don't took / not took ¦ No cogimos el autobús. Fuimos andando. ¦ didn't + forma base.
I met her three years ___ . ¦ ago ¦ before / last / past ¦ La conocí hace tres años. ¦ «hace» + tiempo = … ago (al final).
## food | 🍎 | some, any, much, many | Cuantificadores: some, any, much, many | contables e incontables
Is there ___ milk? ¦ any ¦ some / a / many ¦ ¿Hay leche? ¦ Pregunta → any.
I'd like ___ water, please. ¦ some ¦ any / a / many ¦ Quería agua, por favor. ¦ Petición → some.
How ___ eggs do you need? ¦ many ¦ much / any / lot ¦ ¿Cuántos huevos necesitas? ¦ eggs (contable plural) → many.
How ___ money have you got? ¦ much ¦ many / lot / any ¦ ¿Cuánto dinero tienes? ¦ money (incontable) → much.
We haven't got ___ bread. ¦ any ¦ some / a / no ¦ No tenemos pan. ¦ Negativa → any.
Can I have ___ apple? ¦ an ¦ a / some / any ¦ ¿Me das una manzana? ¦ apple es contable y empieza por vocal → an.
There's ___ cheese in the fridge. ¦ some ¦ a / any / many ¦ Hay queso en la nevera. ¦ cheese es incontable; afirmativa → some.
I don't drink ___ coffee. ¦ much ¦ many / a lot / lots ¦ No bebo mucho café. ¦ Negativa + incontable → much.
There are ___ people in the park. ¦ a lot of ¦ much / a lot / any ¦ Hay mucha gente en el parque. ¦ Afirmativa → a lot of.
I need some ___ about the course. ¦ information ¦ informations / an information / informatio ¦ Necesito información sobre el curso. ¦ information es incontable: sin -s.
## time | 🗓️ | Prepositions of time and place | Preposiciones de tiempo y lugar | at, on, in, under, next to…
The class starts ___ nine o'clock. ¦ at ¦ in / on / – ¦ La clase empieza a las nueve. ¦ Horas → at.
I was born ___ 2001. ¦ in ¦ on / at / – ¦ Nací en 2001. ¦ Años → in.
See you ___ Friday! ¦ on ¦ in / at / the ¦ ¡Nos vemos el viernes! ¦ Días → on.
We go skiing ___ winter. ¦ in ¦ on / at / the ¦ Vamos a esquiar en invierno. ¦ Estaciones → in.
I don't work ___ the weekend. ¦ at ¦ in / on / – ¦ No trabajo el fin de semana. ¦ RU: at the weekend (EE. UU.: on the weekend).
I'm going to visit my aunt ___ week. ¦ next ¦ the next / on next / in next ¦ Voy a visitar a mi tía la semana que viene. ¦ Sin preposición ante next / last / this.
The cat is ___ the table. ¦ under ¦ between / of / at ¦ El gato está debajo de la mesa. ¦ debajo de = under.
The bank is next ___ the post office. ¦ to ¦ of / at / from ¦ El banco está al lado de correos. ¦ next to = al lado de.
My keys are ___ my bag. ¦ in ¦ at / on / of ¦ Mis llaves están en el bolso. ¦ Dentro → in.
I'll see you ___ the evening. ¦ in ¦ at / on / the ¦ Te veo por la tarde. ¦ in the morning / afternoon / evening; at night.
