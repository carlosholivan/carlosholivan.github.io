## formal | 🎩 | Subjunctive and formal fixed phrases | Subjuntivo y fórmulas formales | insist that he be, be that as it may, lest, suffice it to say
The doctor recommended that she ___ for a few days. ¦ rest ¦ rests / rested / will rest ¦ El médico recomendó que descansara unos días. ¦ Subjuntivo: forma base.
It is vital that the patient ___ kept warm. ¦ be ¦ is / was / being ¦ Es fundamental que el paciente se mantenga caliente. ¦ Subjuntivo de be.
They demanded that he ___ the money immediately. ¦ return ¦ returns / returned / would return ¦ Exigieron que devolviera el dinero de inmediato. ¦ Subjuntivo tras demand.
We suggest that the meeting ___ postponed. ¦ be ¦ is / will be / to be ¦ Proponemos que se aplace la reunión. ¦ Subjuntivo pasivo.
Be that as it ___ , we still need an answer today. ¦ may ¦ might / will / can ¦ Sea como fuere, necesitamos una respuesta hoy. ¦ Fórmula fija.
I'll finish this book, come what ___ . ¦ may ¦ might / will / comes ¦ Terminaré este libro, pase lo que pase. ¦ come what may.
___ it to say, the evening was a disaster. ¦ Suffice ¦ Enough / Sufficient / Suffices ¦ Baste decir que la noche fue un desastre. ¦ Suffice it to say.
She kept quiet lest she ___ him. ¦ upset ¦ upsets / would upset / upsetting ¦ Se calló para no disgustarle. ¦ lest + subjuntivo.
If they want to leave, so ___ it. ¦ be ¦ is / will / let ¦ Si quieren irse, que así sea. ¦ so be it.
Heaven ___ that we should ever need it. ¦ forbid ¦ forbids / forbade / forbidding ¦ Dios no quiera que lo necesitemos nunca. ¦ Heaven forbid.
## fronting | 🎭 | Fronting and stylistic inversion | Anteposición e inversión de estilo | Such was, So … was, Into the room came, Try as I might
___ was the noise that I couldn't sleep. ¦ Such ¦ So / Very / Too ¦ Tal era el ruido que no podía dormir. ¦ Such was + sustantivo.
So ___ was the soup that nobody could eat it. ¦ salty ¦ salt / salted / saltiness ¦ Tan salada estaba la sopa que nadie pudo comérsela. ¦ So + adjetivo + was.
Into the room ___ a man in a long black coat. ¦ walked ¦ he walked / did walk / walking ¦ En la habitación entró un hombre con un largo abrigo negro. ¦ Inversión tras lugar.
Here ___ . Get ready! ¦ they come ¦ come they / do they come / they coming ¦ Aquí vienen. ¡Preparaos! ¦ Con pronombre no hay inversión.
Try as she ___ , she couldn't open the jar. ¦ might ¦ may to / did / could have ¦ Por más que lo intentaba, no conseguía abrir el bote. ¦ Try as + sujeto + might.
Strange ___ it may seem, I love the rain. ¦ as ¦ so / how / though it ¦ Por extraño que parezca, me encanta la lluvia. ¦ Adj. + as + sujeto + verbo.
She speaks four languages, as ___ her brother. ¦ does ¦ is / has / speaks it ¦ Habla cuatro idiomas, igual que su hermano. ¦ as + auxiliar + sujeto.
At the top of the hill ___ a ruined castle. ¦ stands ¦ it stands / is standing it / standing ¦ En lo alto de la colina se alza un castillo en ruinas. ¦ Inversión tras lugar.
This much I ___ tell you: she wasn't there. ¦ can ¦ do / am / will to ¦ Esto sí te lo puedo decir: ella no estaba allí. ¦ Anteposición del objeto.
Not once ___ he thank us for our help. ¦ did ¦ had / was / does he ¦ Ni una sola vez nos dio las gracias por la ayuda. ¦ Not once + did.
## absolute | 🧾 | Absolute and participle clauses | Cláusulas absolutas y de participio | weather permitting, having been warned, that said
___ permitting, we'll set off at dawn. ¦ Weather ¦ Time / Climate / Sun ¦ Si el tiempo lo permite, saldremos al amanecer. ¦ Weather permitting.
___ been warned twice, he had no excuse. ¦ Having ¦ Being / Had / Have ¦ Como ya le habían avisado dos veces, no tenía excusa. ¦ Having been + participio.
The speeches ___ , the guests began to dance. ¦ over ¦ overing / overed / were over then ¦ Terminados los discursos, los invitados empezaron a bailar. ¦ Cláusula absoluta.
All things ___ , it was a good decision. ¦ considered ¦ considering / consider / considerate ¦ Bien mirado, fue una buena decisión. ¦ All things considered.
That ___ , there are still a few problems. ¦ said ¦ saying / says / say ¦ Dicho esto, todavía hay algunos problemas. ¦ That said.
Strictly ___ , you need a permit for this. ¦ speaking ¦ spoken / speak / to speak ¦ En sentido estricto, necesitas un permiso para esto. ¦ Strictly speaking.
There ___ no further questions, the meeting ended. ¦ being ¦ been / is / was ¦ Al no haber más preguntas, la reunión terminó. ¦ There being.
___ by his accent, he's from the north. ¦ Judging ¦ Judged / Judge / To judge it ¦ A juzgar por su acento, es del norte. ¦ Judging by.
Not ___ read the instructions, I made a mess of it. ¦ having ¦ had / being / have ¦ Como no había leído las instrucciones, lo hice fatal. ¦ Not having + participio.
Her hands ___ , she opened the letter. ¦ trembling ¦ trembled / tremble / were trembling ¦ Con las manos temblorosas, abrió la carta. ¦ Cláusula absoluta con participio presente.
## prepositions | 📎 | Prepositional phrases and dependent prepositions | Locuciones y preposiciones dependientes | in the light of, by virtue of, conducive to, tantamount to

She got the job by ___ of her experience. ¦ virtue ¦ reason / cause / light ¦ Consiguió el trabajo en virtud de su experiencia. ¦ by virtue of.
We were given vouchers in ___ of a refund. ¦ lieu ¦ place / stead of / view ¦ Nos dieron vales en lugar de un reembolso. ¦ in lieu of.
The species is on the ___ of extinction. ¦ verge ¦ edge / limit / side ¦ La especie está al borde de la extinción. ¦ on the verge of.
A quiet room is conducive ___ concentration. ¦ to ¦ for / with / of ¦ Una habitación tranquila favorece la concentración. ¦ conducive to.
His refusal was tantamount ___ a resignation. ¦ to ¦ with / of / as ¦ Su negativa equivalía a una dimisión. ¦ tantamount to.
Pay will be commensurate ___ qualifications. ¦ with ¦ to / of / for ¦ El sueldo estará en consonancia con la titulación. ¦ commensurate with.
She seemed oblivious ___ the danger. ¦ to ¦ with / at / for ¦ Parecía ajena al peligro. ¦ oblivious to.
They moved to the coast with a view to ___ a business. ¦ opening ¦ open / opened / be opening ¦ Se mudaron a la costa con vistas a abrir un negocio. ¦ with a view to + -ing.
___ any delays, we'll arrive at six. ¦ Barring ¦ Pending / Save of / Lest ¦ Salvo retrasos, llegaremos a las seis. ¦ Barring = salvo.
## reporting | 🗞️ | Reporting verbs and stance | Verbos de habla y postura | purport, concede, dismiss as, imply / infer, allegedly
The letter ___ to come from the bank, but it was a fake. ¦ purported ¦ pretended / alleged / claimed of ¦ La carta pretendía venir del banco, pero era falsa. ¦ purport to.
He finally ___ that he had made a mistake. ¦ conceded ¦ refuted / dismissed / extolled ¦ Por fin admitió que se había equivocado. ¦ concede = admitir.
She dismissed the rumours ___ nonsense. ¦ as ¦ like / for / of ¦ Desestimó los rumores tachándolos de tonterías. ¦ dismiss sth as.
What exactly are you ___ ? That I'm lying? ¦ implying ¦ inferring / referring / deducing ¦ ¿Qué estás insinuando exactamente? ¿Que miento? ¦ imply = insinuar.
I ___ from her silence that she was upset. ¦ inferred ¦ implied / insinuated / alleged ¦ Deduje por su silencio que estaba disgustada. ¦ infer = deducir.
The singer ___ married in secret last month. ¦ allegedly ¦ alleged / allegations / is alleging ¦ Supuestamente, la cantante se casó en secreto el mes pasado. ¦ allegedly = supuestamente.
The minister ___ his comments the next day. ¦ retracted ¦ reiterated / divulged / extolled ¦ El ministro se retractó de sus comentarios al día siguiente. ¦ retract = retirar lo dicho.
He refused to ___ the name of his source. ¦ divulge ¦ retract / concede / imply ¦ Se negó a revelar el nombre de su fuente. ¦ divulge = revelar.
She ___ her innocence throughout the trial. ¦ maintained ¦ conceded / retracted / dismissed ¦ Sostuvo su inocencia durante todo el juicio. ¦ maintain = sostener.
The company is said ___ millions last year. ¦ to have lost ¦ to lose / losing / that it lost ¦ Se dice que la empresa perdió millones el año pasado. ¦ Infinitivo perfecto.
