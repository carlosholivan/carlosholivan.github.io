## tenses | ⏱️ | Advanced tenses | Tiempos verbales avanzados | past perfect continuous, future continuous / perfect, habits
By the time we arrived, they ___ for over an hour. ¦ had been waiting ¦ were waiting / have been waiting / waited ¦ Para cuando llegamos, llevaban más de una hora esperando. ¦ Duración hasta un momento pasado → past perfect continuous.
This time next week I ___ on a beach in Greece. ¦ 'll be lying ¦ 'll lie / 'm lying / 'll have lain ¦ La semana que viene a esta hora estaré tumbado en una playa de Grecia. ¦ En curso en un momento futuro → future continuous.
By 2030, scientists ___ a cure for the disease. ¦ will have found ¦ will find / will be finding / have found ¦ Para 2030, los científicos habrán encontrado una cura. ¦ Terminado antes de un momento futuro → future perfect.
He's always ___ his phone at dinner. It's so annoying! ¦ checking ¦ checks / check / checked ¦ ¡Siempre está mirando el móvil durante la cena! ¡Qué pesado! ¦ always + continuo = hábito molesto.
I ___ to get headaches when I don't sleep enough. ¦ tend ¦ use / am used / would ¦ Suelo tener dolor de cabeza cuando no duermo lo suficiente. ¦ tend to = soler.
Her eyes were red because she ___ . ¦ had been crying ¦ cried / has cried / was cry ¦ Tenía los ojos rojos porque había estado llorando. ¦ Causa de algo visible → past perfect continuous.
The plane is ___ to land at 17.45. ¦ due ¦ bound / about / likely that ¦ Está previsto que el avión aterrice a las 17:45. ¦ be due to = estar previsto.
Don't call at seven. We'll ___ dinner. ¦ be having ¦ have had / having / have ¦ No llames a las siete. Estaremos cenando. ¦ will be + -ing.
I was on the point of ___ when she called. ¦ leaving ¦ leave / to leave / left ¦ Estaba a punto de irme cuando llamó. ¦ on the point of + -ing.
No sooner ___ the meeting started than the fire alarm went off. ¦ had ¦ has / did / was ¦ Nada más empezar la reunión, saltó la alarma de incendios. ¦ No sooner had + sujeto + participio.
## modals | 🕵️ | Modal perfects | Modales en pasado | must have, can't have, should have, needn't have
The road is wet. It ___ rained last night. ¦ must have ¦ must / can't have / should have ¦ La carretera está mojada. Debió de llover anoche. ¦ Deducción segura sobre el pasado.
She ___ seen me. She didn't say hello. ¦ can't have ¦ mustn't have / shouldn't have / didn't must ¦ No puede haberme visto. No me saludó. ¦ Deducción negativa → can't have.
You ___ told me! I would have helped. ¦ should have ¦ must have / would have / could had ¦ ¡Me lo deberías haber dicho! Te habría ayudado. ¦ Reproche → should have.
I ___ bought so much food. Nobody came. ¦ needn't have ¦ mustn't have / couldn't have / didn't need ¦ No hacía falta comprar tanta comida. No vino nadie. ¦ Lo hice y no era necesario.
They ___ got lost. They don't know the area. ¦ might have ¦ must have to / can have / should had ¦ Puede que se hayan perdido. No conocen la zona. ¦ Posibilidad → might have.
You ___ killed yourself! Be more careful. ¦ could have ¦ can have / must / should ¦ ¡Podrías haberte matado! Ten más cuidado. ¦ Posibilidad no realizada → could have.
I ___ go to work yesterday, so I stayed in bed. ¦ didn't need to ¦ needn't have / mustn't / shouldn't have ¦ Ayer no tenía que ir a trabajar, así que me quedé en la cama. ¦ No era necesario (y no lo hice).
He ___ have left already. His coat is still here. ¦ can't ¦ mustn't / shouldn't / needn't ¦ No puede haberse ido ya. Su abrigo sigue aquí. ¦ can't have.
She ___ have studied harder. She failed. ¦ ought to ¦ must / had better / need ¦ Debería haber estudiado más. Suspendió. ¦ ought to have = should have.
The thief ___ have had a key. Nothing was broken. ¦ must ¦ can't / should / ought ¦ El ladrón debía de tener llave. No había nada roto. ¦ Deducción segura.
## conditionals | 🔀 | Conditionals and unreal past | Condicionales y pasado irreal | mixtos, inversión, wish, it's time
If I had taken that job, I ___ in Paris now. ¦ would be living ¦ would have lived / will live / had lived ¦ Si hubiera aceptado ese trabajo, ahora viviría en París. ¦ Condicional mixto: resultado presente.
___ I known, I would have come earlier. ¦ Had ¦ If / Have / Should ¦ De haberlo sabido, habría venido antes. ¦ Inversión: Had + sujeto + participio.
___ you need any help, please contact reception. ¦ Should ¦ If should / Would / Had ¦ Si necesita ayuda, póngase en contacto con recepción. ¦ Inversión formal: Should you…
I wish you ___ stop shouting. ¦ would ¦ will / did / had ¦ Ojalá dejaras de gritar. ¦ Queja → wish + would.
It's time we ___ a decision. ¦ made ¦ make / will make / have made ¦ Ya es hora de que tomemos una decisión. ¦ it's time + pasado.
I'd rather you ___ smoke in the house. ¦ didn't ¦ don't / won't / not ¦ Preferiría que no fumaras en casa. ¦ I'd rather + persona + pasado.
I wish I ___ to the party. It was boring. ¦ hadn't gone ¦ didn't go / wouldn't go / haven't gone ¦ Ojalá no hubiera ido a la fiesta. Fue aburrida. ¦ Arrepentimiento → past perfect.
You can borrow the car ___ you drive carefully. ¦ provided ¦ unless / otherwise / even ¦ Puedes coger el coche siempre que conduzcas con cuidado. ¦ provided = siempre que.
I'll go ___ it's raining. ¦ even if ¦ unless / provided / as long ¦ Iré aunque llueva. ¦ even if = incluso si.
He talks as if he ___ the boss. ¦ were ¦ is / would be / has been ¦ Habla como si fuera el jefe. ¦ as if + pasado irreal.
## passive | 📰 | Advanced passive | La pasiva avanzada | it is said that, is believed to, have something done
It ___ that the company will close. ¦ is rumoured ¦ rumours / is rumouring / rumoured ¦ Se rumorea que la empresa cerrará. ¦ It is + participio + that.
The suspect is thought ___ the country. ¦ to have left ¦ to leave / leaving / that he left ¦ Se cree que el sospechoso ha salido del país. ¦ Acción anterior → to have + participio.
I'm having my teeth ___ tomorrow. ¦ checked ¦ check / checking / to check ¦ Mañana me revisan los dientes. ¦ have + objeto + participio.
She was ___ a prize for her novel. ¦ awarded ¦ award / awarding / awards ¦ Le concedieron un premio por su novela. ¦ Pasiva con persona como sujeto.
You're ___ to wear a seatbelt. ¦ supposed ¦ suppose / supposing / supposedly ¦ Se supone que hay que llevar el cinturón. ¦ be supposed to.
The bridge is being ___ at the moment. ¦ repaired ¦ repair / repairing / to repair ¦ Están reparando el puente en este momento. ¦ is being + participio.
He got his wallet ___ on the bus. ¦ stolen ¦ steal / stealing / stole ¦ Le robaron la cartera en el autobús. ¦ get + objeto + participio (algo que le pasó).
The new hospital is expected ___ next year. ¦ to open ¦ opening / to be open by / that opens ¦ Se espera que el nuevo hospital abra el año que viene. ¦ is expected to + verbo.
The results will have ___ checked by Friday. ¦ been ¦ be / being / was ¦ Para el viernes se habrán revisado los resultados. ¦ will have been + participio.
Millions of people are said ___ in poverty. ¦ to live ¦ living / that live / live ¦ Se dice que millones de personas viven en la pobreza. ¦ are said to + verbo.
## reporting | 🗣️ | Reporting verbs | Verbos de habla | deny -ing, offer to, persuade sb to, accuse sb of
She denied ___ the money. ¦ taking ¦ to take / take / that took ¦ Negó haber cogido el dinero. ¦ deny + -ing.
He offered ___ me with my bags. ¦ to help ¦ helping / help / me to help ¦ Se ofreció a ayudarme con las maletas. ¦ offer + to.
They persuaded me ___ the job. ¦ to accept ¦ accepting / accept / that I accept ¦ Me convencieron para aceptar el trabajo. ¦ persuade + objeto + to.
She accused him ___ lying. ¦ of ¦ for / to / about ¦ Le acusó de mentir. ¦ accuse of + -ing.
He suggested ___ a taxi. ¦ taking ¦ to take / us to take / take ¦ Sugirió coger un taxi. ¦ suggest + -ing.
I apologised ___ late. ¦ for being ¦ to be / being / of being ¦ Me disculpé por llegar tarde. ¦ apologise for + -ing.
The teacher reminded us ___ our homework. ¦ to do ¦ doing / do / of do ¦ El profesor nos recordó que hiciéramos los deberes. ¦ remind + objeto + to.
He insisted ___ for the meal. ¦ on paying ¦ to pay / paying / in paying ¦ Insistió en pagar la comida. ¦ insist on + -ing.
They blamed me ___ the accident. ¦ for ¦ of / about / with ¦ Me culparon del accidente. ¦ blame sb for.
She threatened ___ the police. ¦ to call ¦ calling / call / of calling ¦ Amenazó con llamar a la policía. ¦ threaten + to.
## relatives | 🧱 | Relative and participle clauses | Relativos y participios | of whom, in which, having done, built in
I have two brothers, both of ___ live abroad. ¦ whom ¦ who / them / which ¦ Tengo dos hermanos, y los dos viven fuera. ¦ Cuantificador + of whom.
This is the room in ___ the treaty was signed. ¦ which ¦ that / where / who ¦ Esta es la sala en la que se firmó el tratado. ¦ Preposición + which.
___ I need is a long holiday. ¦ What ¦ That / Which / The thing what ¦ Lo que necesito son unas largas vacaciones. ¦ what = lo que.
___ finished his work, he went home. ¦ Having ¦ Had / After have / Have ¦ Después de terminar su trabajo, se fue a casa. ¦ having + participio.
___ in 1890, the house needs a lot of repairs. ¦ Built ¦ Building / Having built / Build ¦ Construida en 1890, la casa necesita muchas reparaciones. ¦ Participio con sentido pasivo.
___ knowing the way, we used the GPS. ¦ Not ¦ No / Don't / Without ¦ Como no sabíamos el camino, usamos el GPS. ¦ not + -ing.
Students ___ the course will receive a certificate. ¦ completing ¦ completed / who completing / complete ¦ Los alumnos que terminen el curso recibirán un certificado. ¦ Relativa reducida activa.
The man ___ car was stolen called the police. ¦ whose ¦ who / who's / which ¦ El hombre cuyo coche robaron llamó a la policía. ¦ whose = cuyo.
She bought six books, none of ___ she has read. ¦ which ¦ them / whom / that ¦ Compró seis libros y no ha leído ninguno. ¦ none of which.
It was Tom ___ broke the vase. ¦ who ¦ which / what / whom ¦ Fue Tom quien rompió el jarrón. ¦ Oración escindida: It was… who.
## patterns | 🧩 | Verb patterns and emphasis | Patrones verbales y énfasis | want sb to, make / let, it's not worth, What I…
I want you ___ me the truth. ¦ to tell ¦ tell / telling / that you tell ¦ Quiero que me digas la verdad. ¦ want + objeto + to.
My parents never let me ___ out late. ¦ stay ¦ to stay / staying / stayed ¦ Mis padres nunca me dejaban quedarme fuera hasta tarde. ¦ let + objeto + forma base.
It's not worth ___ the film. ¦ seeing ¦ to see / see / seen ¦ No merece la pena ver la película. ¦ it's not worth + -ing.
I couldn't help ___ at his joke. ¦ laughing ¦ to laugh / laugh / laughed ¦ No pude evitar reírme con su chiste. ¦ can't help + -ing.
I object ___ treated like a child. ¦ to being ¦ to be / being / for being ¦ Me niego a que me traten como a un niño. ¦ object to + -ing (gerundio pasivo).
He seems ___ his keys. He can't get in. ¦ to have lost ¦ to lose / losing / having lost ¦ Parece que ha perdido las llaves. No puede entrar. ¦ Infinitivo perfecto.
There's no point ___ about it now. ¦ in worrying ¦ to worry / worry / for worry ¦ No tiene sentido preocuparse por eso ahora. ¦ there's no point (in) + -ing.
I ___ like your new haircut! ¦ do ¦ am / does / did be ¦ ¡Sí que me gusta tu nuevo corte de pelo! ¦ do enfático.
All I want ___ some peace and quiet. ¦ is ¦ are / it is / that ¦ Lo único que quiero es un poco de paz y tranquilidad. ¦ All… is…
The reason ___ I left was the salary. ¦ why ¦ because / for / what ¦ El motivo por el que me fui fue el sueldo. ¦ the reason why.
## vocabulary | 🔤 | Word formation, phrasal verbs and idioms | Formación de palabras, phrasal verbs y modismos | prefijos, sufijos, come up with, piece of cake
It's ___ to drive without a licence. ¦ illegal ¦ unlegal / inlegal / dislegal ¦ Es ilegal conducir sin carné. ¦ il- ante l.
Don't be so ___ . Wait a moment. ¦ impatient ¦ unpatient / inpatient / dispatient ¦ No seas tan impaciente. Espera un momento. ¦ im- ante p.
There has been a great ___ in his behaviour. ¦ improvement ¦ improveness / improvity / improval ¦ Ha mejorado mucho su comportamiento. ¦ improve → improvement.
She came ___ with a brilliant idea. ¦ up ¦ out / on / down ¦ Se le ocurrió una idea genial. ¦ come up with = idear.
I can't put up ___ this noise any longer. ¦ with ¦ to / for / on ¦ Ya no aguanto más este ruido. ¦ put up with = aguantar.
The company had to lay ___ fifty workers. ¦ off ¦ out / down / up ¦ La empresa tuvo que despedir a cincuenta trabajadores. ¦ lay off = despedir.
The test was a piece of ___ . ¦ cake ¦ pie / bread / sugar ¦ El examen estaba chupado. ¦ a piece of cake = muy fácil.
That new phone costs an arm and a ___ . ¦ leg ¦ hand / eye / foot ¦ Ese móvil nuevo cuesta un ojo de la cara. ¦ cost an arm and a leg.
There was ___ rain all night. ¦ heavy ¦ strong / big / hard ¦ Llovió mucho toda la noche. ¦ Colocación: heavy rain.

## discourse | 🧷 | Discourse markers and quantifiers | Marcadores del discurso y cuantificadores | as far as, after all, hardly any, a great deal of
As far as I'm ___ , it's a great idea. ¦ concerned ¦ concerning / concern / regarded ¦ Por lo que a mí respecta, es una gran idea. ¦ as far as I'm concerned.
Don't be too hard on him. ___ all, he's only a child. ¦ After ¦ Above / At / In ¦ No seas tan duro con él. Al fin y al cabo, solo es un niño. ¦ after all = al fin y al cabo.
The flight was cheap. ___ , the seats were uncomfortable. ¦ Mind you ¦ Despite / Although / Whereas ¦ El vuelo fue barato. Eso sí, los asientos eran incómodos. ¦ mind you = eso sí (matiz).
There's ___ any coffee left. ¦ hardly ¦ nearly / almost / barely not ¦ Casi no queda café. ¦ hardly any.
It took a great ___ of effort. ¦ deal ¦ lot / number / amount of ¦ Hizo falta muchísimo esfuerzo. ¦ a great deal of.
___ of the people I know agree with you. ¦ Most ¦ The most / Most of / Almost ¦ La mayoría de la gente que conozco está de acuerdo contigo. ¦ most of + the / my…
In ___ of price, the first option is better. ¦ terms ¦ term / regard / respect of ¦ En cuanto al precio, la primera opción es mejor. ¦ in terms of.
The plan was expensive and, ___ , difficult to carry out. ¦ moreover ¦ however / whereas / despite ¦ El plan era caro y, además, difícil de llevar a cabo. ¦ moreover = además.
By the ___ , did you call your mother? ¦ way ¦ time / means / chance ¦ Por cierto, ¿llamaste a tu madre? ¦ by the way = por cierto.
I'm in the right place, ___ I? ¦ aren't ¦ am not / amn't / isn't ¦ Estoy en el sitio correcto, ¿no? ¦ I'm…, aren't I?
