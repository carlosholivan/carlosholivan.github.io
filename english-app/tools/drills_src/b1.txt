## narrative | 📖 | Narrative tenses and used to | Tiempos narrativos y used to | pasado simple, continuo, perfecto; used to, would
When we got to the cinema, the film ___ . ¦ had started ¦ has started / started / was starting ¦ Cuando llegamos al cine, la película ya había empezado. ¦ Ocurrió antes de llegar → past perfect.
She ___ in the garden when it started to rain. ¦ was working ¦ worked / had worked / has worked ¦ Estaba trabajando en el jardín cuando empezó a llover. ¦ Acción en curso interrumpida → pasado continuo.
I ___ to have long hair when I was at university. ¦ used ¦ use / would / was used ¦ En la universidad llevaba el pelo largo. ¦ Estado pasado → used to (no would).
I didn't ___ to like vegetables. ¦ use ¦ used / using / uses ¦ Antes no me gustaban las verduras. ¦ didn't use to.
Every Sunday we ___ visit our grandparents. ¦ would ¦ were / had / did use ¦ Todos los domingos visitábamos a los abuelos. ¦ Hábito repetido → would.
He was nervous because he ___ flown before. ¦ had never ¦ has never / never / didn't ¦ Estaba nervioso porque nunca había volado. ¦ Experiencia anterior a otro pasado → past perfect.
It took me months to get used to ___ on the left. ¦ driving ¦ drive / drove / have driven ¦ Tardé meses en acostumbrarme a conducir por la izquierda. ¦ get used to + -ing.
By the time I arrived, everybody ___ home. ¦ had gone ¦ went / has gone / was going ¦ Para cuando llegué, todos se habían ido a casa. ¦ by the time → past perfect.
I ___ my keys, so I couldn't get in. ¦ had lost ¦ have lost / was losing / lose ¦ Había perdido las llaves, así que no pude entrar. ¦ Causa anterior → past perfect.
Did you ___ to play any sports at school? ¦ use ¦ used / using / would ¦ ¿Hacías algún deporte en el colegio? ¦ Did you use to…?
## perfect | ⏳ | Present perfect: simple or continuous | Present perfect: simple o continuo | for, since, just, so far; vs past simple
I've been waiting here ___ two o'clock. ¦ since ¦ for / from / at ¦ Llevo esperando aquí desde las dos. ¦ since + momento.
She ___ three books this year. ¦ has written ¦ has been writing / wrote / writes ¦ Este año ha escrito tres libros. ¦ Cantidad terminada → present perfect simple.
You're covered in paint! What ___ ? ¦ have you been doing ¦ have you done / did you do / are you doing ¦ ¡Estás lleno de pintura! ¿Qué has estado haciendo? ¦ Actividad reciente con resultado visible → continuo.
We ___ in this house for ten years. ¦ have lived ¦ live / are living / lived since ¦ Vivimos en esta casa desde hace diez años. ¦ Duración hasta ahora → present perfect.
I ___ him at a party in 2015. ¦ met ¦ have met / have been meeting / had met ¦ Lo conocí en una fiesta en 2015. ¦ Momento terminado → pasado simple.
I've known her ___ years. ¦ for ¦ since / during / from ¦ La conozco desde hace años. ¦ for + periodo.
When ___ you buy that jacket? ¦ did ¦ have / had / do ¦ ¿Cuándo te compraste esa chaqueta? ¦ when → pasado simple.
How long ___ you been learning English? ¦ have ¦ are / do / did ¦ ¿Cuánto llevas aprendiendo inglés? ¦ How long have you been…?
So far we ___ any problems. ¦ haven't had ¦ didn't have / don't have / hadn't ¦ Hasta ahora no hemos tenido ningún problema. ¦ so far → present perfect.
I ___ this film three times! ¦ 've seen ¦ 've been seeing / saw ever / see ¦ ¡He visto esta peli tres veces! ¦ Número de veces → simple.
## future | 🔭 | Future forms and probability | El futuro y la probabilidad | will, going to, presente continuo, might, likely

The concert ___ at 8 p.m. ¦ starts ¦ will starting / is start / start ¦ El concierto empieza a las 8 de la tarde. ¦ Horario → presente simple.
We ___ dinner with Tom tonight. We booked a table. ¦ 're having ¦ have / will have to / had ¦ Esta noche cenamos con Tom. Hemos reservado mesa. ¦ Plan organizado → presente continuo.
She'll ___ get the job. She's the best candidate. ¦ probably ¦ likely / may / possible ¦ Probablemente consiga el trabajo. Es la mejor candidata. ¦ will probably.
It's ___ to snow this weekend. ¦ likely ¦ probably / maybe / might ¦ Es probable que nieve este fin de semana. ¦ be likely to.
I ___ think he'll come. ¦ don't ¦ not / won't / doesn't ¦ No creo que venga. ¦ I don't think + will.
Look at the score! They're ___ win. ¦ going to ¦ will / might to / going ¦ ¡Mira el marcador! Van a ganar. ¦ Predicción con pruebas → going to.
— The phone's ringing. — I ___ get it. ¦ 'll ¦ 'm going to / get / 'm getting ¦ —Suena el teléfono. —Ya lo cojo yo. ¦ Decisión instantánea → will.
I'm not sure. I ___ go to the party. ¦ may not ¦ don't may / mayn't / not may ¦ No estoy seguro. Puede que no vaya a la fiesta. ¦ may not.
She definitely ___ be late. She's never late. ¦ won't ¦ doesn't / isn't / not will ¦ Seguro que no llega tarde. Nunca llega tarde. ¦ definitely won't.
## conditionals | 🔀 | Conditionals and wishes | Condicionales y wish | cero, primero, segundo, tercero; I wish

If you heat ice, it ___ . ¦ melts ¦ would melt / melted / will melted ¦ Si calientas hielo, se derrite. ¦ Verdad general → condicional cero.
If it ___ tomorrow, we'll cancel the trip. ¦ rains ¦ will rain / rained / would rain ¦ Si llueve mañana, cancelaremos la excursión. ¦ Primer condicional: if + presente.
What would you do if you ___ a million euros? ¦ won ¦ win / would win / had won ¦ ¿Qué harías si ganaras un millón de euros? ¦ Segundo condicional: if + pasado.
If I had known, I ___ you. ¦ would have told ¦ would tell / will tell / had told ¦ Si lo hubiera sabido, te lo habría dicho. ¦ Tercer condicional: would have + participio.
If we ___ earlier, we wouldn't have missed the train. ¦ had left ¦ left / would leave / have left ¦ Si hubiéramos salido antes, no habríamos perdido el tren. ¦ if + past perfect.
I wish I ___ more free time. ¦ had ¦ have / would have / will have ¦ Ojalá tuviera más tiempo libre. ¦ wish + pasado.
I wish I ___ that. I'm so sorry. ¦ hadn't said ¦ didn't say / haven't said / wouldn't say ¦ Ojalá no hubiera dicho eso. Lo siento mucho. ¦ Arrepentimiento → wish + past perfect.
I'll lend you the car ___ you drive carefully. ¦ as long as ¦ unless / even if / if not ¦ Te presto el coche siempre que conduzcas con cuidado. ¦ as long as = siempre que.
We won't go out ___ it stops raining. ¦ unless ¦ if / as long as / when not ¦ No saldremos a menos que deje de llover. ¦ unless = a menos que.
## modals | ⚖️ | Modals: obligation, permission, deduction | Modales: obligación, permiso, deducción | must, have to, allowed to, must be, can't be

You ___ bring anything. There's plenty of food. ¦ don't need to ¦ mustn't / needn't to / haven't ¦ No hace falta que traigas nada. Hay comida de sobra. ¦ No es necesario.
Are we ___ to use dictionaries in the exam? ¦ allowed ¦ allow / let / permit ¦ ¿Se puede usar diccionario en el examen? ¦ be allowed to.
You'd better ___ now or you'll be late. ¦ leave ¦ to leave / leaving / left ¦ Más vale que te vayas ya o llegarás tarde. ¦ had better + forma base.
She's been working all day. She ___ be exhausted. ¦ must ¦ can't / mustn't / should to ¦ Lleva todo el día trabajando. Debe de estar agotada. ¦ Deducción segura → must.
That ___ be Tom. He's on holiday in Peru. ¦ can't ¦ mustn't / must / might ¦ Ese no puede ser Tom. Está de vacaciones en Perú. ¦ Deducción negativa → can't.
I'm not sure where she is. She ___ be at the gym. ¦ might ¦ must / can't / should ¦ No sé dónde está. Puede que esté en el gimnasio. ¦ Posibilidad → might.
Yesterday we ___ stay late at the office. ¦ had to ¦ must / have to / musted ¦ Ayer tuvimos que quedarnos hasta tarde en la oficina. ¦ Pasado de must → had to.
You ___ to see a doctor about that cough. ¦ ought ¦ should / must / had better ¦ Deberías ir al médico por esa tos. ¦ ought to (con to).
You ___ smoke in here. It's against the law. ¦ mustn't ¦ don't have to / needn't / don't need ¦ Aquí no se puede fumar. Va contra la ley. ¦ Prohibición → mustn't.
## passive | 🏭 | The passive | La voz pasiva | is made, was built, has been sold, will be done
This castle ___ in the 13th century. ¦ was built ¦ built / is built / was build ¦ Este castillo se construyó en el siglo XIII. ¦ Pasiva en pasado.
Rice ___ in many Asian countries. ¦ is grown ¦ grows / is grow / grown ¦ El arroz se cultiva en muchos países asiáticos. ¦ Pasiva en presente.
My bike ___ stolen! ¦ has been ¦ has / is been / was being ¦ ¡Me han robado la bici! ¦ Present perfect pasivo.
The results will ___ published on Friday. ¦ be ¦ been / being / are ¦ Los resultados se publicarán el viernes. ¦ will be + participio.
The kitchen is ___ painted at the moment. ¦ being ¦ been / be / was ¦ Están pintando la cocina en este momento. ¦ is being + participio.
This song was written ___ a 15-year-old girl. ¦ by ¦ from / of / with ¦ Esta canción la escribió una chica de 15 años. ¦ Agente → by.
English ___ all over the world. ¦ is spoken ¦ speaks / is speak / spoken ¦ El inglés se habla en todo el mundo. ¦ Pasiva refleja española → pasiva inglesa.
The form must ___ in black ink. ¦ be completed ¦ complete / completed / being completed ¦ El formulario debe rellenarse con tinta negra. ¦ modal + be + participio.

## relatives | 🔗 | Relative clauses | Oraciones de relativo | who, which, whose, where; con y sin comas
My sister, ___ lives in Rome, is a doctor. ¦ who ¦ that / which / whose ¦ Mi hermana, que vive en Roma, es médica. ¦ Explicativa con persona → who (nunca that).
That's the restaurant ___ we had our first date. ¦ where ¦ which / that / when ¦ Ese es el restaurante donde tuvimos la primera cita. ¦ Lugar → where.
He's the writer ___ novels have been translated into 30 languages. ¦ whose ¦ who / who's / which ¦ Es el escritor cuyas novelas se han traducido a 30 idiomas. ¦ whose = cuyo.
She passed her driving test, ___ surprised everyone. ¦ which ¦ that / what / who ¦ Aprobó el examen de conducir, lo cual sorprendió a todos. ¦ which = lo cual.
The book ___ I'm reading is fascinating. ¦ that ¦ who / what / whose ¦ El libro que estoy leyendo es fascinante. ¦ Cosa → that / which (o nada).
Do you remember the day ___ we met? ¦ when ¦ where / which / who ¦ ¿Te acuerdas del día en que nos conocimos? ¦ Tiempo → when.
I need someone ___ can speak Japanese. ¦ who ¦ which / whose / where ¦ Necesito a alguien que hable japonés. ¦ Persona → who.
The Thames, ___ flows through London, is 346 km long. ¦ which ¦ that / who / where ¦ El Támesis, que atraviesa Londres, mide 346 km. ¦ Explicativa con cosa → which.
What's the name of the man ___ car you crashed into? ¦ whose ¦ who / that / which ¦ ¿Cómo se llama el hombre contra cuyo coche chocaste? ¦ Posesión → whose.
That's all ___ I know. ¦ that ¦ what / which / who ¦ Eso es todo lo que sé. ¦ Tras all → that (✗ all what).
